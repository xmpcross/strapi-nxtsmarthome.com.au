The eufyCam 2C Pro is a wire-free outdoor security camera system that operates in conjunction with eufy's HomeBase 2 hub. According to eufy's Australian specifications, the cameras capture video in 2K (1440p) resolution across a 135-degree diagonal field of view, incorporating both infrared night vision and an integrated spotlight for colour night footage. Rather than connecting directly to your domestic router, the cameras communicate via a dedicated wireless link to the HomeBase 2, which includes 16 GB of non-expandable local eMMC storage. This setup avoids ongoing monthly cloud subscription fees. Each camera runs on an internal 6,700 mAh rechargeable battery rated by the manufacturer for up to 180 days between charges, recharging through a Micro-USB port.

## Who it suits

This system suits Australian homeowners and tenants who want outdoor monitoring without recurring cloud costs or fixed electrical wiring. Because the cameras carry an IP67 weather-resistance rating and communicate wirelessly with the hub, they can be positioned around exterior walls, carports, or gardens. The on-device detection technology identifies human shapes to minimise false alerts from passing animals or windblown plants, as stated by eufy. However, households requiring continuous 24/7 video capture should look elsewhere, as battery constraints limit recording to motion-triggered clips. Property owners needing extensive video archives may also find the non-expandable 16 GB base storage restrictive if multiple cameras log frequent events.

## Australian notes

The HomeBase 2 is mains-powered using an included 5V/2A DC power adapter fitted with a standard Australian plug. It connects to your network via a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi connection or wired Ethernet. Major Australian retailers including JB Hi-Fi and The Good Guys supply the kit with a 12-month manufacturer warranty. For smart home setups, eufy confirms compatibility with Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa, but there is no confirmed support for Matter, Samsung SmartThings, or official Home Assistant integration. Note that Apple HomeKit caps the camera stream at 1080p rather than native 2K. If you plan to install cameras on boundary fences or shared rental properties, check your state or territory surveillance legislation regarding privacy and recording public spaces, and consult tenancy rules before drilling exterior fixtures.
