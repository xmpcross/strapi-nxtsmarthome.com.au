The ENGLAON ANYWHERE 32-inch 4K UHD Touch Screen (model LED32P90-4K) is a mobile smart monitor mounted on an omnidirectional wheeled floor stand. According to ENGLAON, the unit features a 3840 × 2160 resolution LED panel with an INCELL 10-point capacitive touchscreen, supporting tilt adjustment and 90-degree auto-rotation between portrait and landscape modes. The display runs Google-certified Android 13 powered by a Rockchip RK3588S octa-core processor, 8GB of RAM, and 128GB of onboard storage. An integrated 136.8Wh lithium-ion battery provides up to five hours of cord-free operation, while built-in dual 5W speakers, HDMI 2.0, and USB ports allow playback from external sources.

## Who it suits
This display suits Australian renters and homeowners wanting a flexible screen that moves between living areas, home offices, and bedrooms without wall brackets. The portrait pivoting function suits vertical video formats, digital reading, and fitness apps, while the responsive touchscreen eliminates the immediate need for a remote, keyboard, or mouse.

However, it will not suit brightly lit rooms or outdoor alfresco areas. ENGLAON rates panel brightness at 200 nits, which struggles against heavy daylight and glare. It is also unsuitable for high-refresh competitive gaming, as the display is limited to a 60Hz refresh rate and a 16ms response time. At 19.2kg including the base, the unit rolls easily on flat indoor floors but is cumbersome to carry up and down stairs. Those looking for traditional broadcast television should note that it lacks an integrated DVB-T tuner, meaning live free-to-air channels must be viewed via streaming apps or an external tuner connected over HDMI.

## Australian notes
ENGLAON supplies the unit with a standard Australian 240V mains plug and a 90W external power adapter to recharge the internal battery. Local retailer listings confirm the monitor is covered by a 12-month Australian manufacturer warranty. Wireless networking supports dual-band Wi-Fi 6 across both 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands, as well as Bluetooth 5.1. While the system runs Google-certified Android 13 with Google Play access and Chromecast functionality, manufacturer data does not list native smart home dashboard controls or integration with Matter, Apple Home, Alexa, or Home Assistant.
