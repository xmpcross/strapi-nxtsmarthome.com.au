The Ring Indoor Cam (2nd Gen) is a compact, mains-powered security camera intended solely for indoor residential use. According to Ring Australia, the camera records in 1080p HD resolution with a 143-degree diagonal field of view and includes Colour Night Vision, two-way audio with noise cancellation, customisable motion zones, and a 72 dB siren. A prominent hardware feature highlighted by Ring and local retailers is its manual swivelling privacy cover, which mechanically blocks the camera lens and cuts power to the internal microphone when turned into place.

## Who it suits

This camera suits homeowners and apartment dwellers who already use Amazon Alexa speakers and smart displays, as Ring specifies full "Works with Alexa" integration for viewing live feeds and hearing motion alerts. It is also practical for people seeking a flexible plug-in device that can sit on flat surfaces or mount to a wall using the included bracket. If you are renting and plan to mount hardware or position cameras in shared living areas, check your state or territory residential tenancy agreement and privacy guidelines.

It is not suited to households looking for standalone, subscription-free video storage. According to Ring Australia, the camera lacks an onboard microSD card slot or network video recorder (NVR) compatibility. While basic functions like Live View and real-time motion notifications work without payment, recording clips and accessing cloud video history requires a paid Ring Protect subscription after the 30-day trial ends. Buyers whose smart homes centre on Apple Home, Google Home, or Matter should also look elsewhere, as manufacturer listings confirm the camera does not support these platforms natively.

## Australian notes

According to Ring Australia, the Indoor Cam (2nd Gen) includes an indoor standard Australian 5V, 2A power adapter and a 1.9-metre Micro-USB cable. The device contains no internal battery backup, meaning it must stay continuously connected to an active mains power outlet. 

Network connectivity is restricted to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n); Ring Australia confirms 5 GHz Wi-Fi is unsupported. Retailers list the camera with a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty, which sits alongside your non-excludable consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law. When positioning cameras inside a home, review your state or territory surveillance device legislation to ensure compliance regarding recording conversations and indoor visual privacy.
