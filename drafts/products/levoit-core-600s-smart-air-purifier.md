The Levoit Core 600S Smart Air Purifier is a floor-standing cylindrical cleaner designed for expansive residential spaces. According to Levoit, the purifier achieves a Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) of 697 m³/h using a three-stage filter configuration. This system combines an outer pre-filter for dust and lint, an H13 True HEPA filter that Levoit states captures 99.97% of airborne particles down to 0.3 micrometres, and an activated carbon layer to help neutralise household odours. A built-in AirSight Plus laser particle sensor monitors ambient air conditions in real time, projecting numerical PM2.5 measurements onto an upper digital display while modulating fan speeds when set to Auto Mode. Wi-Fi connectivity allows configuration through the VeSync mobile app.

## Who it suits

The Core 600S suits households with open-plan living, dining, and kitchen zones that need rapid air turnover to clear dust, pet dander, or seasonal bushfire haze. Levoit states the unit can cycle room air five times per hour across spaces up to approximately 60 m², or twice per hour across larger areas up to 147 m². It also suits homes integrated into Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant smart ecosystems, allowing hands-free voice toggles alongside scheduled routines created within the VeSync platform.

Prospective buyers should skip this unit if their setup relies on Apple Home or the Matter standard, as neither protocol is supported out of the box. Those looking for an unobtrusive bedroom cleaner may also find its 60 cm height and 6.2 kg weight overly bulky for smaller spaces. While Levoit specifies a quiet sleep mode down to 26 dB, top fan speeds reach up to 55 dB, which may prove distracting in quiet rooms during high-particulate clearing cycles.

## Australian notes

Australian stock carries the model identifier HEAPAPLVSAU0137Y across local retailers including JB Hi-Fi, Bing Lee, and Harvey Norman. The Australian configuration includes a 220–240 V power supply fitted with an Australian mains plug, along with a 24-month manufacturer express warranty.

Connecting the purifier to the VeSync application requires a dedicated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; 5 GHz bands are unsupported. Ongoing maintenance requires changing the cylindrical all-in-one replacement filter cartridge (model HEACAFLVNUS0061A), which Levoit recommends replacing every 6 to 8 months depending on household run-time and pollution exposure. For enthusiasts running local home automation platforms, community-developed VeSync integrations enable monitoring and automated controls within Home Assistant.
