The WiZ Smart Colour Bulb A60 B22 is an 8.5W smart LED globe produced by Signify for standard Australian bayonet fittings. According to manufacturer specifications, it outputs 806 to 810 lumens—matching a traditional 60W incandescent globe—and offers both full RGB colour and tunable white spectrums from 2200K warm white to 6500K cool daylight. The bulb connects directly over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth without requiring a separate hardware bridge, and Signify rates its expected operational lifespan at up to 25,000 hours.

## Who it suits

This bulb suits renters and property owners who want colour and white ambiance adjustments without altering existing light fixtures. Because it uses the standard B22 base and requires no hardware bridge, it fits typical Australian pendant and lamp fittings with minimal configuration. It is well suited to multi-platform smart homes: Matter certification allows direct integration into Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, and Home Assistant. It also suits bedrooms and living areas where scheduling warmer light in the evening and cooler white during the day is helpful. It does not suit fixtures controlled by hardwired dimmer dials, nor homes that run exclusively 5 GHz Wi-Fi networks without a backward-compatible 2.4 GHz band.

## Australian notes

The globe features a standard B22 bayonet cap configured for Australian 220–240V AC mains at 50/60 Hz. Because it simply inserts into existing bayonet fittings, installation does not involve electrical work. However, Signify explicitly notes that this smart globe must not be used on circuits fitted with traditional wall dimmers, as hardware dimmers can interfere with the internal electronics; dimming must instead be handled via the WiZ app, compatible voice assistants, or smart switches. If you have existing hardwired dimmers and plan to alter your wall switches, check your state's electrical safety regulator regarding mandatory licensed electrical work. The bulb is rated for indoor use only and comes backed by a two-year manufacturer warranty in Australia.
