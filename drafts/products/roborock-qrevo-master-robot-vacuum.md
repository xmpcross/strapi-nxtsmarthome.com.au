The Roborock Qrevo Master (model RR-QRM52-03-BLK) is an autonomous robot vacuum and mop combining 10,000 Pa suction with dual spinning mop pads operating at 200 RPM. According to Roborock Store ANZ, the unit features an extendable FlexiArm side brush and right-side mop extension designed to reach edges and room corners. The included Multifunctional Dock 3.0 automates daily maintenance by washing the mop pads with 60°C hot water, drying them with 45°C warm air, refilling the onboard water tank, and emptying the dustbin into a disposable bag rated for up to seven weeks of storage. Floor navigation combines PreciSense LiDAR with an RGB camera using Reactive AI obstacle detection.

## Who it suits

According to retailer and manufacturer data, this unit suits households with large areas of hard flooring or a mix of hard surfaces and low-pile carpets. Households with shedding pets benefit from the DuoRoller Riser system, which Roborock states uses counter-rotating rubber brushes to minimise hair wrapping. It also suits smart-home users seeking multi-platform flexibility. Manufacturer and integration documentation confirms support for Matter (via firmware version 02.27.40 or newer), which enables integration with Apple Home and SmartThings, alongside Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and an official native Home Assistant integration.

Households with thick, plush, or deep-pile carpets may want to look at alternative solutions. Specifications indicate the mop lifts by 10 mm over floor coverings, which may not provide adequate clearance to keep wet pads from touching deeper carpet fibres. Additionally, users looking for fully automated dock maintenance should note that this model does not include an automatic detergent dispenser, meaning cleaning solution must be manually measured into the clean water tank. Floor areas with loose phone cables will still need clearing beforehand, as user reports indicate thin cords can still be caught.

## Australian notes

According to Roborock Australia and authorised retailers, local units are built for 220V–240V AC power (50/60 Hz) and supply a standard Australian power cord. Official Australian stock carries a 24-month manufacturer warranty. Connecting the vacuum to your home network requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi band, as the hardware is incompatible with 5 GHz-only network setups. Roborock does not charge a subscription for its mobile application, allowing free access to multi-floor mapping, no-go zones, and custom scheduling.
