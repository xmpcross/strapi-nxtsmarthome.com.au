The Roborock Qrevo Edge C (model RR-QREC02-03-WHT) is a robotic vacuum and mop featuring an automated cleaning and maintenance dock. According to manufacturer specifications, the robot delivers 18,500 Pa suction and utilises a DuoDivide dual roller brush designed to reduce hair tangles. For hard surfaces, it deploys dual spinning mopping pads running at up to 200 RPM alongside a FlexiArm extending edge mop to reach along skirting boards. The included Multifunctional Dock 3.0 handles post-cleaning tasks automatically, emptying dry debris into a 2.5-litre dust bag, washing the mopping pads with 75°C hot water, and drying them with warm air. Navigation is handled by a PreciSense LiDAR sensor paired with structured-light Reactive Tech obstacle avoidance.

## Who it suits

This model suits Australian households with mixed flooring such as tiles, timber, and carpets. The 10 mm automatic mop lift allows it to vacuum low-pile carpets while mopping adjoining hard floors in a single cycle. Pet owners will appreciate the dual anti-tangle roller system and high suction capacity. However, buyers should skip this unit if their home contains high internal door sills or room transitions over standard heights; according to Reviews.org AU, the Edge C lacks the motorised AdaptiLift chassis found on the regular Qrevo Edge that clears obstacles up to 30 mm. Additionally, those who want remote home monitoring or obstacle photo identification should look elsewhere, as the structured-light system does not feature an RGB camera or live video streaming. Households that prefer built-in voice commands without third-party smart speakers will also miss the "Hello Rocky" voice assistant present on higher-tier models.

## Australian notes

Australian retail listings confirm the Qrevo Edge C ships with a standard 220–240 V Australian power plug and carries a two-year manufacturer warranty through local retailers. Network setup requires a dedicated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi band, as 5 GHz connections are not supported. Smart-home integration is broad, featuring Matter compatibility alongside Google Home and Amazon Alexa support. Apple Home users can control the vacuum through Matter or Apple Siri Shortcuts, though native HomeKit functionality outside of Matter is not supported. Roborock confirms that no subscription fees are required to access full mapping, docking functions, or scheduling features.
