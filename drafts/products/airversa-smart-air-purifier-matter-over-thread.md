The Airversa Purelle AP2 is a compact smart air purifier designed specifically for the Apple Home ecosystem. Unlike many smart climate appliances that connect over standard 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, the AP2 contains no Wi-Fi radio and communicates strictly using Thread and Bluetooth LE. According to Airversa, the unit utilises a three-stage filtration setup featuring a pre-filter, an H13 True HEPA filter (with an optional H14 PRO upgrade), and an activated carbon filter to capture 99.97% of airborne particles down to 0.3 microns. It features an onboard PM2.5 sensor, a digital display, and a five-colour air quality LED indicator ring, providing a Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) of 221 m³/h. While early product materials referenced Matter, the AP2 does not support Matter over Thread.

## Who it suits
This air purifier suits Apple Home users looking for a bedroom, study, or nursery purifier that integrates directly into iOS without requiring third-party app subscriptions. With Airversa recommending a room size of 28 square metres (or filtering spaces up to roughly 93 square metres across a 60-minute window), it is suited to contained rooms rather than open-plan layouts. Households with an existing Apple Thread network can control fan speeds, set timers, and read PM2.5 levels locally without loading their home network with additional Wi-Fi traffic.

It does not suit Android users, nor does it suit homes built around Amazon Alexa, Google Home, or Samsung SmartThings, as cross-platform smart home support is absent. Anyone who lacks an Apple Thread Border Router—such as an Apple HomePod mini or an Apple TV 4K with Thread—should also look elsewhere, as Thread communication and out-of-home control depend on one.

## Australian notes
The Purelle AP2 operates on standard Australian mains voltages, with its power supply rated for 100–240 V AC at 50/60 Hz. The unit draws 24 W during normal operation and 0.5 W on standby, powered via an external barrel-style power adapter. Buyers should confirm with their retailer that an Australian-compliant wall plug is supplied in the box.

While Airversa does not operate a direct local web storefront, the unit is available through Australian distributors like Clever House, and replacement filter consumables are stocked by retailers such as JB Hi-Fi. The purifier is backed by a 12-month manufacturer warranty alongside consumer protections provided under Australian Consumer Law.
