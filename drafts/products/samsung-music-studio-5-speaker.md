The Samsung Music Studio 5 (models HW-LS50H in black and HW-LS51H in white) is a mains-powered 2.0-channel wireless speaker designed for tabletop and bookshelf placement. According to Samsung Australia, the unit features three integrated drivers comprising a built-in woofer and front-firing tweeters with an acoustic waveguide. The manufacturer states that it supports Dolby Atmos, Dolby Digital Plus, and 24-bit/96 kHz Hi-Res Audio playback, alongside room tuning via SpaceFit Sound Pro and AI Dynamic Bass Control. The speaker measures 251.0 × 283.5 × 137.0 mm, weighs 2.4 kg, and offers wireless connectivity alongside a single digital optical input.

## Who it suits

According to Samsung's specifications, this speaker suits households already invested in the Samsung hardware ecosystem. If you own a compatible 2022 to 2026 Samsung television, the brand confirms you can use Q-Symphony to output sound across both the television and speaker drivers at once. It also suits listeners wanting multi-platform wireless streaming, as Samsung lists support for Apple AirPlay 2, Google Cast, and built-in Amazon Alexa voice control.

Conversely, users looking for a portable speaker for outdoor entertaining or cordless room-to-room listening should pass, as retailer specifications confirm the Music Studio 5 has no battery and requires continuous mains power. Those planning a home-theatre setup with HDMI eARC will also find it limited, as wired television connection is restricted to an optical audio port. Furthermore, smart-home users seeking native Matter, Apple HomeKit, or Home Assistant control will find these platforms absent from Samsung's published specifications.

## Australian notes

Samsung Australia supplies the Music Studio 5 with a standard Australian 10A power plug and lead designed for 220–240V mains supplies at 50/60 Hz. Data from Harvey Norman and Samsung states an operating power consumption of 15 W, with standby power rated at 0.5 W. For local network setups, the speaker connects to both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi 6 bands, though Samsung notes that the 5 GHz band is mandatory if you plan to group multiple units using Group Play. Australian retailer listings, including JB Hi-Fi, confirm the speaker comes with a 12-month standard manufacturer warranty.
