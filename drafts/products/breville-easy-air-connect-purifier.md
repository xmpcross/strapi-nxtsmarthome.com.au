The Breville Easy Air Connect Purifier (model LAP158) is a compact desktop air cleaner tailored for personal spaces and small rooms up to 25 square metres. According to Breville Australia, the unit employs a 360-degree bottom air inlet to draw air through four stages of purification. These comprise a pre-filter for larger dust particles, an activated carbon filter for household odours, a True HEPA filter rated to capture 99.97 per cent of particles down to 0.3 micrometres, and a switchable Microbe Shield UV-C light. Operating via an included 220–240V plug-in power adapter with a 12W power rating, the unit features four fan speeds including a quiet Night Mode, an integrated nightlight, and timer durations for two, four, or eight hours.

## Who it suits

This purifier suits Australian renters, students, and homeowners who need targeted air cleaning in compact areas such as studies, nurseries, or small bedrooms. Because it weighs 1.52 kilograms and stands 321 millimetres high, it fits neatly onto a desk or bedside table without monopolising floor space. Users who prefer remote scheduling will find value in the Breville Home Connect app, which provides automation and basic voice integration via Google Assistant or Amazon Alexa without any subscription fees.

Conversely, those looking to purify medium-to-large living areas or open-plan homes should look elsewhere. Breville states a Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) of up to 91 cubic metres per hour, which cannot adequately cycle air in large rooms. Buyers who want an air purifier that automatically adjusts its fan speed according to current pollution levels should also skip this model, as it omits the live SensAir air quality sensors included on Breville's higher-tier units.

## Australian notes

Breville packages the LAP158 with an Australian 220–240V mains adapter and covers the hardware with a local two-year replacement warranty. For network connectivity, the unit's Wi-Fi hardware functions exclusively on 2.4 GHz bands. If your home Wi-Fi router combines 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz under a single network name, you may need to configure your router to present a distinct 2.4 GHz network during initial pairing. Smart platform compatibility is restricted to Google Home and Amazon Alexa; Apple Home, Matter, and Samsung SmartThings are not supported. Furthermore, Breville’s documentation notes that the internal Microbe Shield UV-C lamp is not user-replaceable, meaning the appliance must be taken to an authorised service centre when the globe requires maintenance.
