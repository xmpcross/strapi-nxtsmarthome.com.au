The Ecovacs DEEBOT T80S OMNI (model DEX62) is a robot vacuum and mop designed for automated residential floor cleaning. According to Ecovacs Australia, the unit delivers up to 24,800 Pa suction alongside the OZMO ROLLER 2.0 system, which uses a continuous self-cleaning roller mop spinning at 220 RPM with 3,800 Pa downward pressure. Obstacle recognition and path planning are driven by an embedded Mini-ToF laser sensor paired with AIVI 3D 3.0, which combines an RGB AI camera with structured light. Its companion OMNI station handles maintenance by washing the roller with 75°C hot water, drying it with 63°C hot air, and emptying dry debris into an internal 3 L dust bag.

## Who it suits
This appliance suits households with mixed floor finishes seeking hands-off floor upkeep. Ecovacs states that the ZeroTangle 3.0 dual-comb roller brush system actively limits hair wrap, making it appropriate for homes dealing with pet hair or longer strands. The TruEdge 3.0 roller extends outward to reach within 1.5 cm of skirting boards, and the robot can climb internal door thresholds up to 20 mm. Routine controls, scheduling, multi-level mapping, and no-go zones function without any paid subscription. However, households with low-clearance furniture should note the robot stands 98 mm tall. It may not suit those wanting fully automated chemical cleaning, as the station lacks an automatic detergent dispenser, and the 2.2 L dirty water tank holds substantially less than the 4 L clean tank, requiring more frequent manual emptying. Furthermore, CyberShack notes that floor-length mirrors and reflective surfaces can confuse its optical mapping sensors.

## Australian notes
Units sold by Australian retailers such as Bing Lee, The Good Guys, and Harvey Norman include an Australian Type I three-pin mains plug and operate on standard 220–240 V AC power. Local stock includes a 24-month manufacturer warranty. For network connectivity, the DEEBOT T80S OMNI operates strictly on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n) and does not support 5 GHz networks, which may require separating router bands during setup. It is certified for Matter over Wi-Fi, allowing integration into Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, and Home Assistant. Because the robot features an active camera for optical navigation, households subject to rental agreements or shared tenancies should review local camera surveillance rules.
