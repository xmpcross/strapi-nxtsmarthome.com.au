The Dreame Aqua10 Ultra Roller Robot Vacuum Cleaner (model RLH71DE) is an automated wet-and-dry floor cleaner combining a continuous-cleaning roller mop with up to 30,000 Pa Vormax suction, according to Dreame Technology Australia. Its multi-function dock houses a 3.2 L auto-emptying dust bag alongside a 4 L clean-water tank and a 3.5 L dirty-water tank. Dreame specifies that the dock cleans the roller using 100°C hot water before initiating hot-air drying. Navigation combines an OmniSight AI 3D obstacle avoidance camera with a VersaLift retractable LDS LiDAR turret, which lowers the unit's height to roughly 98 mm to access low furniture.

## Who it suits

According to manufacturer and retailer data, this model suits busy households with extensive hard flooring that need active wet mopping for spills rather than passive damp wiping. Homes with raised architectural transitions between rooms benefit from the ProLeap threshold system, which Dreame states can clear single steps up to 4.2 cm and staged steps up to 8 cm. For mixed hard-floor and rug layouts, the AutoSeal mechanism lifts the wet roller 14 mm and encloses it to prevent water transfer onto carpet.

Conversely, households with plush or high-pile carpeting should exercise caution. Owner reviews on ProductReview.com.au indicate that the robot can struggle on thick carpets or wedge itself between rugs and walls, with limited manual reverse options. Additionally, independent analysis by Vacuum Wars indicates that the robot's cautious obstacle-avoidance logic results in slower overall room coverage compared to standard spinning-pad vacuum models. The roller ends and bearings also require manual clearing if long pet or human hair accumulates.

## Australian notes

According to the Dreame user manual, the Aqua10 Ultra dock is rated for 220–240 V at 50/60 Hz and ships with a standard Australian 230V 3-pin mains plug. Australian retailers, including JB Hi-Fi and The Good Guys, provide a 3-year manufacturer warranty.

Network setup requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi connection, as 5 GHz bands are unsupported. For smart home control, the robot is CSA Matter certified, facilitating integration into Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Samsung SmartThings. There is currently no official out-of-the-box Home Assistant integration, though community options exist. All remote controls, mapping, and video feeds operate without a mandatory paid subscription.
