The Xiaomi Smart Air Purifier 6 (model AC-M25-SC) is an upright indoor air purifier designed for medium-to-large single rooms and apartment living areas up to 50 square metres. According to Xiaomi specifications, the unit delivers a Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) of 443 cubic metres per hour for particulate matter and uses a multi-stage composite filtration system. This design incorporates a primary pre-filter for coarse debris, a high-efficiency filter trapping 99.98% of 0.3-micron particles, an activated carbon layer for volatile organic compounds and household odours, internal ultraviolet-C (UVC) sterilisation, and a negative ion generator. An onboard colour LCD displays real-time metrics captured by five integrated sensors that track PM1, PM2.5, coarse dust, ambient temperature, and relative humidity.

## Who it suits

This purifier suits homeowners and renters wanting automated indoor air filtration without needing to set up separate air-quality monitors. Because Xiaomi specifies an effective coverage area between 29 and 50 square metres, it fits open-plan living rooms, master bedrooms, and combined dining spaces well. Households managing seasonal pollen, pet dander, or bushfire smoke will benefit from the five-sensor detection and the multi-layer composite filter. Those already using the Xiaomi Home app, Google Home, or Amazon Alexa can automate schedules and adjust fan speeds using routines alongside existing smart home devices.

Conversely, users looking for native Apple Home or Matter integration should look elsewhere, as Xiaomi limits out-of-the-box voice assistant support to Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa. Buyers wanting a compact bedside unit or a whole-home filtration system should also consider alternative models, as this floor-standing unit weighs roughly 5.2 kilograms and stands 55.5 centimetres high.

## Australian notes

The purifier operates on standard 200–240V mains power at 50/60Hz with a rated consumption of 40W, matching Australian residential electrical supply. When buying through Australian retailers, ensure the unit is an Australian model supplied with an AS/NZS 3112 plug and Regulatory Compliance Mark (RCM) certification. If purchased as a grey-market parallel import, units may arrive with overseas plugs and require setting the Xiaomi Home app server to China Mainland, which can cause connection issues with Australian smart speakers. The purifier connects solely via standard 2.4GHz Wi-Fi networks and will not pair with 5GHz-only Wi-Fi bands. As a free-standing plug-in appliance, it needs no property alterations, which suits renters.
