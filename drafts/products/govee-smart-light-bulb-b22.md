The Govee Smart Light Bulb B22 is an RGBWW LED globe designed to fit standard Australian bayonet fittings. According to official distributor Clever Gear and local retailer listings, the bulb draws 9W of power (0.5W on standby) and produces between 800 and 1,000 lumens of brightness. It features an adjustable white colour temperature spectrum ranging from 2700K to 6500K alongside over 16 million colours and 54 preset scene modes. While Govee does not host an Australian manufacturer site, its products are distributed locally through Clever Gear and major retailers.

## Who it suits

This bulb suits Australian renters and homeowners seeking app- or voice-controlled colour lighting without needing to install extra bridges or modify light switches. Because it connects directly over Wi-Fi, it works well in standalone fixtures, floor lamps, or bedside fittings. Buyers using Apple Home, Google Home, or Amazon Alexa will find it convenient if they purchase the Matter-enabled variant (model H6004E0B). It is also suitable for users who want automated daily schedules, such as sunrise and sunset lighting routines configured through the Govee Home app.

It is not suitable for light circuits connected to traditional wall dimmers, as manufacturer notes state that physical dimmer switches will cause issues and dimming must be handled through software. Households looking for autonomous sound-reactive lighting should also note that music synchronisation relies on an active smartphone microphone within the Govee app rather than an on-bulb audio sensor. Furthermore, SmartThings and Home Assistant integrations are not confirmed on official Australian spec sheets.

## Australian notes

The bulb uses a B22 bayonet base and is rated for Australian mains power at 220–240V at 50/60Hz. It requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi band, meaning dual-band router setups may need separate network names or temporary 5 GHz disabling during initial pairing.

Local buyers must check packaging carefully when shopping at retailers like JB Hi-Fi and Harvey Norman. The product exists in two main B22 variants in Australia: model H6004E0B includes Matter and Apple Home support, whereas model H6004EC1 is limited to standard Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Google Home, and Alexa. Clever Gear listings specify a 12-month manufacturer express warranty, which sits alongside consumer rights under Australian Consumer Law. The unit is rated strictly for indoor use.
