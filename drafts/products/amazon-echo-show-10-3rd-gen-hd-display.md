The Amazon Echo Show 10 (3rd Gen, model T4E4AT) is a smart display built around a motorised 10.1-inch touchscreen base. Amazon states that the display uses a 13 MP wide-angle camera alongside motion processing powered by an Amazon AZ1 Neural Edge processor to pan horizontally across a 350-degree arc. This mechanism is designed to keep the screen and camera centred on you during video calls, recipe viewing, or video streaming. Audio is delivered through an integrated 2.1 sound system consisting of two 1.0-inch tweeters and a 3.0-inch woofer. Inside, the device incorporates a Zigbee radio and operates as a Matter controller over Wi-Fi and Thread, enabling direct pairing with compatible smart home accessories without a third-party bridge.

## Who it suits

This display suits households configured around Amazon Alexa that want a central countertop device for hands-free video calling, kitchen timers, and smart home management. Amazon notes that the auto-framing camera and rotating screen suit busy spaces like kitchens, where you move between work areas while viewing recipes or speaking over Zoom, Skype, or Alexa calling. It also suits users wanting to manage Zigbee and Matter devices directly from a single unit. Conversely, it is not suitable for homes running Apple Home or Google Home, as neither platform is supported natively. It should also be skipped if you intend to place it on a narrow bookshelf or crowded counter, as the motorised screen requires clear, dedicated space around its perimeter to rotate freely without striking nearby objects.

## Australian notes

Official Australian stock available through retailers such as JB Hi-Fi, The Good Guys, and Officeworks includes a 30W external power adapter with an Australian mains plug and an approximate 1.5-metre cable. Wireless networking supports dual-band 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11a/b/g/n/ac). While core Alexa commands, standard calling, and local device control do not require a subscription, streaming platforms such as Netflix, Spotify, and Prime Video require separate paid accounts. Amazon provides remote camera access through the Alexa app to monitor the room while away; homeowners and renters using this feature should check their state or territory surveillance device legislation regarding indoor camera monitoring. The product is covered by a one-year manufacturer limited warranty alongside statutory guarantees under Australian Consumer Law.
