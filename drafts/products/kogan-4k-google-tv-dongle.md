The Kogan 4K Google TV Dongle (model KAGDSX5MA) is a compact streaming device designed to plug directly into a display via HDMI. According to Kogan's technical documentation, the unit runs certified Google TV OS and is powered by an ARM Cortex-A35 64-bit quad-core processor with 2GB of DDR4 RAM and 8GB of internal eMMC storage. It delivers 4K Ultra HD output with HDR support and includes Dolby Audio decoding. For connectivity, the dongle features dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0, and integrated Chromecast capability for media streaming from phones and tablets.

## Who it suits

This streaming dongle suits renters or homeowners who want to modernise a secondary television, computer monitor, or older panel that lacks modern streaming software. The Google TV interface provides access to popular streaming platforms such as Netflix, Disney+, YouTube, and Amazon Prime Video, while the included remote features Google Assistant voice input for search and smart device controls. It is also well-suited to households already using the Google Home ecosystem for shared media casting.

It is less suited to users who require extensive storage for numerous heavy applications or local media libraries, as the 8GB total capacity leaves modest space after system software is accounted for. Those requiring a wired Ethernet connection for home networks should also look elsewhere, as the unit relies solely on Wi-Fi out of the box. Additionally, the manufacturer documentation confirms no native integration with Apple Home, Matter, or Samsung SmartThings.

## Australian notes

Kogan supplies the dongle with a standard Australian AC power adapter and a USB-A to Micro-USB power cable. Product documentation specifies that the device requires 5V/1A (5W) input and should be powered via the wall plug, as standard television USB ports often fail to supply enough current for reliable 4K playback.

The unit operates on both 2.4 GHz and 5.0 GHz Wi-Fi frequencies, ensuring compatibility with standard Australian home routers and National Broadband Network (NBN) gateway setups. While there are no recurring subscription fees required to operate the hardware or the Google TV interface, paid streaming subscriptions remain separate. For local purchaser support, Kogan provides a 1-year manufacturer warranty alongside standard protections under Australian Consumer Law.
