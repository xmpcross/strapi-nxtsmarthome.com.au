The SwitchBot Plug Mini is a compact, single-outlet smart adapter designed for scheduling, automation routines, and appliance control. According to SwitchBot, the device connects directly to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Low Energy, allowing users to toggle connected hardware remotely, establish operational timers, and track power consumption via the SwitchBot application without an ongoing subscription. Depending on the regional model, SwitchBot confirms compatibility with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home, SmartThings, and Matter (either natively or when bridged via a SwitchBot Hub 2).

## Who it suits

Based on published specifications, this accessory is designed for residences using North American electrical systems (100–125V) seeking appliance automation and voice control across platforms such as Alexa or Google Home. For Australian households, however, it suits nobody. SwitchBot does not manufacture an Australian Type I model, and the brand's authorised Australian distributor does not range it. Listings found on cross-border retail channels are grey-market imports that cannot safely operate in Australian homes. Australian buyers looking for smart plugs with energy monitoring should look instead at locally approved, 230–240V alternatives with native three-pin plugs.

## Australian notes

Australian domestic mains power operates on a nominal 230–240V AC supply at 50 Hz through three-pin Type I wall outlets. SwitchBot's technical documentation indicates that the manufactured Plug Mini is rated strictly for 100–125V AC at 50/60 Hz and features North American Type A or Type B pins. Attempting to power a 100–125V appliance on Australian 230–240V mains presents a significant fire and electrical failure risk.

Furthermore, imported units lack the Regulatory Compliance Mark (RCM) required for mains-connected equipment sold in Australia. SwitchBot's local distributor, Clever Gear, does not stock the Plug Mini, meaning international units sold by third parties carry no official Australian warranty support. Check your state's electrical safety regulator regarding compliance rules and safety obligations before attaching any imported mains adapter to domestic wiring.

In terms of connectivity, the unit operates only on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n) networks and does not support 5 GHz bands. While SwitchBot states that Matter compatibility exists on specific iterations, energy reporting data is limited to the SwitchBot app and does not bridge over Matter.
