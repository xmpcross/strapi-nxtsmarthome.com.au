The LIFX Clean Antibacterial Smart Bulb E27 (model LHLA19E27UVAU) is an Edison-screw smart LED that combines everyday ambient lighting with germicidal surface treatment. LIFX states that the bulb delivers up to 1200 lumens of brightness with a colour rendering index (CRI) of 80, offering tunable white from 1500K to 9000K as well as a full RGB colour gamut. Its central distinguishing feature is a 405nm High Energy Visible (HEV) cleaning mode. According to LIFX and research cited by the company, this HEV light cycle targets bacteria such as *Staphylococcus aureus* and *Escherichia coli*. The manufacturer states that this wavelength is safe for people, pets, and plants, operating directly over home Wi-Fi without needing a dedicated hardware bridge.

## Who it suits

This bulb suits homeowners and renters who want to schedule bacterial cleaning over targeted surfaces such as kitchen benches, desks, or bathroom vanity areas. Because it draws 11.5W at full output (0.5W in standby) and provides high white light brightness, it works well in task lamps or close-proximity overhead fixtures where automated schedules can run while the space is unoccupied. However, households should be aware of physical limitations noted by LIFX: disinfection efficacy depends strictly on distance and exposure time. Shorter cycles require close placement, whereas larger coverage areas require prolonged exposure. Buyers seeking broad virucidal protection should skip this model, as regulatory disclosures confirm it is antibacterial only and carries no antiviral ratings. Users who need wide, ambient diffusion may also find the 135-degree beam angle more directional than typical omnidirectional globes.

## Australian notes

The bulb is configured for Australian power, rated for AC 100–240V at 50/60 Hz. Because it uses a standard E27 screw base, installation is completely non-invasive and requires no licensed electrical work. LIFX provides a two-year limited warranty for Australian purchases, and core automations do not require a paid subscription. For home networking, the bulb connects solely to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n), so dual-band routers must broadcast a standard 2.4 GHz SSID. LIFX confirms platform support for Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, and Home Assistant, though Matter is not supported on this model.
