The Nanoleaf Essentials Smart Bulb E27 (2-Pack) is a set of multi-colour, tunable white smart LED globes designed for standard Edison screw fixtures. According to Nanoleaf, each A60 globe uses a distinct faceted geometric shape and draws 9 watts of power, delivering an average brightness of 900 lumens with a peak of up to 1,000 lumens. The bulbs produce over 16 million colours alongside adjustable white light ranging from 2700K warm white to 6500K daylight, featuring a Colour Rendering Index (CRI) rated at 90 or higher. For smart connectivity, the globes operate over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi using the Matter standard, allowing direct app control or voice assistant operation without requiring a subscription.

## Who it suits
According to retailer and manufacturer details, this two-pack suits Australian households looking to automate standard screw-in lamps or ceiling sockets across multi-platform smart homes. Because Nanoleaf includes Matter support, the bulbs suit users running Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, or Home Assistant, provided an appropriate Matter hub is already present on the local network. They also suit renters and homeowners wanting automated colour temperature adjustments, as Nanoleaf features Circadian Lighting to shift white light tones throughout the day.

Conversely, buyers should skip these bulbs if their fixtures are wired to traditional in-wall dimmer switches, as Nanoleaf notes the hardware requires full continuous mains power and must only be dimmed via software or compatible wireless switches. They are also not intended for outdoor fittings due to an indoor-only rating, nor will they work in bayonet (B22) fittings without an adapter.

## Australian notes
The Essentials E27 bulbs are rated for Australian mains electricity at 220–240V AC at 50Hz and fit standard E27 Edison screw sockets. They require a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network for operation; dual-band routers must have an active 2.4 GHz band because 5 GHz-only connections are unsupported. Initial onboarding uses Bluetooth to assist pairing.

Local retailer listings from JB Hi-Fi, Bunnings, and Officeworks confirm the twin pack carries a two-year manufacturer warranty in Australia. Because these are screw-in globes, installation does not involve hardwiring; if you need to replace a damaged fitting or alter fixed wiring, check your state's electrical safety regulator first.
