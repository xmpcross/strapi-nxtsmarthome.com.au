The Dyson Purifier Cool (TP07) is a floor-standing tower appliance that combines air purification with a bladeless oscillating fan. Dyson states that the machine incorporates a fully sealed HEPA H13 filter and activated carbon layer designed to capture 99.95% of particles down to 0.1 microns, including dust, pollen, and domestic odours. Built-in environmental sensors analyse indoor air quality in real time, displaying particulate and gas metrics on the front-facing LCD screen and adjusting airflow automatically when set to auto mode. The unit provides 10 fan speed settings, up to 350-degree oscillation, and a diffused backward-airflow mode that maintains air filtration without directing cool air forward.

## Who it suits

This unit suits apartment dwellers, renters, and homeowners who want a combined air purifier and personal cooling fan in a single footprint. It is well suited to bedrooms and home offices where automated scheduling, lower night-mode noise, and app-based air quality tracking are beneficial. Allergy sufferers who want continuous automated air quality monitoring will also find the multi-sensor feedback useful.

Conversely, households looking for rapid, high-volume air cleaning across expansive open-plan living areas should consider dedicated high-CADR air purifiers, as the TP07 delivers a CADR rating of 94 cfm at top speed. Those seeking a room-cooling appliance should also note that Dyson explicitly classifies this unit as a fan rather than an air conditioner; it circulates ambient air but does not reduce ambient room temperature. Buyers should also verify model naming before purchasing: while the TP07 includes Wi-Fi connectivity and app support, Dyson's similar Autoreact (TP7A) model omits Wi-Fi and app features entirely.

## Australian notes

The TP07 operates on standard Australian 230V mains power with a 1.8-metre power cord and standard 10A plug. Dyson Australia backs the unit with a two-year manufacturer warranty and local customer support.

For smart home connectivity, the TP07 connects over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi (using Bluetooth 4.0 for initial pairing) and interfaces with the MyDyson app on compatible iOS and Android devices. Dyson supports voice control through Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa, as well as Siri Shortcuts on Apple devices, though it lacks native Apple Home (HomeKit) or Matter support. Community-maintained integrations are available for Home Assistant. The included magnetic remote control contains a button battery; check Product Safety Australia guidelines regarding button battery precautions around young children.
