The Philips Hue Discover Outdoor Floodlight is a mains-powered exterior wall light designed to illuminate outdoor areas such as patios, driveways, and garden entertaining spaces. According to Philips Hue Australia, the luminaire consumes 15 W of power and delivers up to 2,250 lumens at 4,000 K (or 2,050 lumens at 2,700 K). As part of the White and Colour Ambiance line, it supports 16 million colours as well as tunable white illumination ranging between 2,000 K and 6,500 K. The fixture head can pivot horizontally and tilt vertically, allowing users to direct the beam. The fitting does not connect directly to home Wi-Fi; instead, it uses Zigbee to communicate via a Philips Hue Bridge.

## Who it suits
This floodlight suits homeowners who already use the Philips Hue ecosystem or plan to build a Zigbee-based smart lighting network. Because platform integration—including Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, and Matter—relies on a Philips Hue Bridge (sold separately), the fitting is best matched to households with that hardware in place. It is well suited to outdoor entertainment zones where homeowners want the flexibility to switch between bright cool-white task lighting and coloured ambient scenes.

It is less suited to renters or temporary dwellings. Because it is a permanent, hardwired 240 V fixture rather than a plug-in low-voltage fitting, it cannot be easily removed or repositioned. Users looking for standalone floodlights that connect straight to home Wi-Fi without a bridge will also need to consider other models. Additionally, the IP44 rating protects against splashing water from any angle, but it is not built for high-pressure water jets or severe exposed conditions where IP65-rated hardware is standard.

## Australian notes
In Australia, the Philips Hue Discover is supplied as a 220 V to 240 V (50–60 Hz) Class I earthed unit with no standard domestic plug or external lead. Because this fitting is hardwired to mains, installation is generally work for a licensed electrician; check your state's electrical safety regulator for local wiring rules.

Because the light uses Zigbee rather than direct 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, outdoor placement depends on Zigbee mesh coverage linking back to your Hue Bridge or nearby mains-powered Hue lights. Philips Hue Australia backs the fitting with a two-year manufacturer warranty, and app scheduling, automations, and remote control do not require a paid subscription.
