The Philips Hue Play Light Bar Double Pack is a mains-powered indoor accent lighting kit designed for entertainment spaces and ambient backlighting. According to the Australian manufacturer specifications, the pack includes two White and Color Ambiance light bars, table stands, and brackets that allow the units to sit horizontally, stand vertically, or mount to the back of a television or monitor. Operating at a combined 13.2 watts, the lights deliver up to 500 lumens each at 4000K across 16 million colours and tunable white shades ranging between 2000K and 6500K.

## Who it suits

These light bars suit renters and homeowners wanting adjustable bias lighting behind a computer monitor or TV without altering walls or fittings. Because the fixtures rest on stands or attach to hardware via brackets, no drilling is needed, which makes them practical for rental spaces.

They are best suited to users who already own or plan to install a Philips Hue Bridge. While basic single-room control is possible via Bluetooth, full platform integration and scheduling require the bridge. PC users can synchronise the bars with display content using desktop software, but living room users should note that synchronising with television HDMI content requires purchasing a separate Philips Hue Play HDMI Sync Box or a compatible smart TV application. Those wanting a direct Wi-Fi fitting without additional equipment should skip this pack, as the bars communicate over Zigbee and Bluetooth. With an IP20 rating, they are not designed for outdoor or wet areas.

## Australian notes

Retail listings from JB Hi-Fi, Officeworks, and Bunnings confirm that Australian stock includes a local Type I power adapter designed for standard 50–60 Hz Australian mains outlets. The power supply incorporates three connection ports, which according to Philips Hue allows households to run up to three Play units from a single wall outlet to minimise powerboard clutter.

The hardware is classified as a Class III Safe Extra Low Voltage fixture. When paired to a Philips Hue Bridge, the setup links into Matter, Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Samsung SmartThings. The specifications state that the lights operate without direct Wi-Fi, relying instead on Zigbee communication with the hub or direct Bluetooth app pairing. Philips Hue Australia provides a two-year manufacturer warranty, with an official minimum software support commitment of 60 months from purchase.
