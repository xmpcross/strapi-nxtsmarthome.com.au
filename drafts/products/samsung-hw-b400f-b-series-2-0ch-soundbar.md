The Samsung HW-B400F (Australian model code HW-B400F/XY) is a standalone 2.0-channel soundbar designed for straightforward television audio setups. According to Samsung Australia, the unit houses four built-in speakers, including an integrated woofer, inside a compact enclosure measuring 641 mm wide, 66.5 mm high, and 107 mm deep. Weighing 2.1 kg, it connects to displays via HDMI ARC or an optical port, while also supporting Bluetooth 4.2 and USB playback for MP3, AAC, FLAC, and WAV files. Supported audio processing includes Dolby 2ch, Surround Sound Expansion, Voice Enhance Mode, Night Mode, and LPCM 2ch.

## Who it suits

This soundbar suits apartment dwellers, renters, and secondary-room viewers who want clearer dialogue without cluttering their living area with extra boxes or trailing cables. Because the woofer is built directly into the main bar, it requires no floor space for a separate subwoofer unit. Samsung highlights Voice Enhance Mode to boost spoken clarity and Night Mode to compress dynamic audio spikes, making it practical for late-night viewing in shared housing or compact apartments.

Conversely, home theatre enthusiasts looking for cinematic immersion should look elsewhere. Samsung's specifications confirm that the HW-B400F does not decode Dolby Atmos or DTS:X spatial audio formats, nor does it include separate rear channels. Buyers wanting multi-room music streaming, network playback, or automated voice control will also find it unsuitable, as the hardware completely lacks Wi-Fi.

## Australian notes

In Australia, the HW-B400F includes a 12-month manufacturer warranty, as listed by Samsung Support Australia and local retailers like Officeworks. Samsung states the unit supports Free Voltage, operating at 20 W with a 0.5 W standby draw, though official documentation does not detail the specific Australian plug format or whether it uses an external power brick.

Smart-home builders should be aware that this unit is entirely offline. Product data confirms it has no Wi-Fi connectivity, meaning it will not integrate with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Home Assistant, Matter, or Samsung's own SmartThings ecosystem. It functions purely as a conventional television audio bar managed via standard remotes or HDMI CEC. Renters should also note that Samsung does not include a wall-mount bracket in the box, meaning placement on a console or media cabinet is the default setup.
