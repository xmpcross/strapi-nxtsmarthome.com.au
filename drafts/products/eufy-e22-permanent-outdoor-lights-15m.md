The eufy Permanent Outdoor Light E22 (model T8L02CE1) is a 15-metre mains-powered exterior light string featuring 30 triple-LED nodes. According to eufy Australia, the string uses RGBWW LEDs capable of delivering RGB colour options alongside tuneable white temperatures spanning 1500K to 9000K. The hardware carries an IP65 ingress rating across the light strand and inline controller, while the included outdoor power adapter is rated IP67. Rather than functioning as temporary festive lights, the product is intended for permanent mounting on eaves or exterior walls, with eufy rating the diodes for over 55,000 hours of operation across ambient temperatures from -20°C to 60°C.

## Who it suits

This setup suits Australian homeowners who already use eufy Security cameras, as the brand states the lights can link with cameras to greet visitors or deter intruders with preset illumination. It is also well suited to households using Amazon Alexa or Google Home voice assistants who want year-round architectural and festive lighting without paying recurring subscription fees.

It is less suitable for smart homes built around Apple Home or Matter, as neither standard is supported on the E22 model. It is also unsuitable for installations requiring exact trim lengths; eufy’s manual confirms the light strip cannot be cut to fit shorter runs, meaning excess nodes must be turned off through the app. Renters should note that mounting requires screw clips or exterior adhesive, and should check their tenancy agreement or local tenancy authority regarding exterior property alterations.

## Australian notes

Australian retail units include an exterior power adapter with an Australian standard plug. If your eaves lack an outdoor weatherproof socket, adding one is fixed mains wiring, which is generally work for a licensed electrician; check your state's electrical safety regulator for the rules on outdoor sockets.

For connectivity, the controller requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi connection, with eufy specifying that 5 GHz bands are unsupported. Local retailers, including JB Hi-Fi and Harvey Norman, supply the unit with a 24-month manufacturer warranty, which applies alongside statutory consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law.
