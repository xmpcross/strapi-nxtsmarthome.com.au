The eufy Security SoloCam S340 is an outdoor, wire-free surveillance camera featuring a dual-camera layout and a detachable solar panel. According to manufacturer specifications, the unit combines a 3K wide-angle lens with a 2K telephoto lens to provide dual viewing angles and up to 8x hybrid zoom. The motorised turret provides 360-degree horizontal panning and 70-degree vertical tilting, intended to track movement across open spaces such as driveways and backyards. Captured footage stores directly to 8 GB of onboard eMMC memory without requiring a monthly cloud subscription, though the camera can also link to an optional eufy HomeBase 3 hub for expanded central storage.

## Who it suits
This camera suits homeowners looking to monitor broad perimeter areas without running mains power cables or regularly taking down hardware to recharge batteries. The modular solar panel can sit directly on the camera chassis or be positioned separately using the supplied extension cable to maximise sun exposure. It also suits households already using Google Assistant or Amazon Alexa smart displays who want voice-controlled streaming to their screens.

Conversely, it is less suited to renters who cannot drill mounting holes into exterior surfaces, as the brackets screw into the wall. Users who rely on 5 GHz Wi-Fi or cross-platform ecosystems like Apple Home or Matter will need to look elsewhere, as eufy limits native support to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and its proprietary mobile app. Those seeking non-stop 24/7 video recording should also note that prolonged continuous recording on battery power depends heavily on sunny conditions and is constrained by the built-in 8 GB capacity.

## Australian notes
The SoloCam S340 connects exclusively to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks. Australian homes using modern dual-band or mesh routers will need to ensure a 2.4 GHz band is broadcast and reaches the outdoor installation point. The unit carries an operating temperature rating of -20°C to 50°C, which covers typical Australian summer conditions.

Because this unit features 360-degree motorised panning and covers broad sightlines, positioning requires care. Pointing outdoor surveillance cameras toward neighbouring properties or public footpaths may be subject to surveillance device and privacy laws; consult your state or territory privacy regulator or legal guidelines to verify local requirements. Renters should consult their state residential tenancy authority before drilling brackets into masonry or external cladding. Australian retailers supply this model with a standard 12-month manufacturer warranty.
