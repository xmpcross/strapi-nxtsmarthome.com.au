The Google Nest Audio is a mains-powered smart speaker designed to serve as an audio playback unit and a central voice controller for the Google Home ecosystem. According to Google's specifications, the speaker is equipped with a 75 mm woofer, a 19 mm tweeter, and three far-field microphones. It connects over dual-band Wi-Fi (802.11b/g/n/ac) or Bluetooth 5.0, integrates Chromecast functionality, and supports grouping with other Google Nest speakers as well as stereo pairing with a second Nest Audio unit. A physical toggle on the rear housing disconnects the microphones at the hardware level.

## Who it suits

This speaker suits Australian households that manage compatible lighting, sensors, and appliances through Google Assistant and the Google Home app. It works well for listeners who stream audio hands-free through services like YouTube Music or Spotify, or those who want to use their home speakers as a domestic intercom via Google's broadcast features. Google also notes that the Nest Audio acts as a Matter controller, making it suitable for users wanting local control of Matter-over-Wi-Fi devices without relying on third-party bridge hardware.

It is not suited to users who need a portable speaker for outdoor entertaining, verandas, or camping, as it contains no battery and must stay plugged into mains power. Those invested in Apple Home or Amazon Alexa ecosystems should look elsewhere, as the unit cannot function as an AirPlay target or respond to Siri or Alexa voice inputs. Additionally, smart-home setups relying heavily on Thread accessories should note that while the Nest Audio controls Matter devices over Wi-Fi, it lacks a Thread border router.

## Australian notes

Australian retail stock (models GA01420-AU in Chalk and GA01586-AU in Charcoal) includes an Australian 240V plug pack with a 1.5-metre cable and carries a 12-month manufacturer warranty through major local retailers. Support for both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi bands allows users to keep congested 2.4 GHz frequencies clear for lower-bandwidth IoT devices across standard NBN modems and routers.

Because the device continuously monitors for activation wake words via its far-field array, consider placement in shared spaces or rentals. State and territory surveillance device laws across Australia govern audio recording and listening devices differently depending on location and context; check your state's surveillance legislation if you intend to install voice-listening speakers in tenanted, communal, or commercial environments.
