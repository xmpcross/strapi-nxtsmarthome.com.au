The Yamaha MusicCast 20 (model WX-021) is a mains-powered wireless speaker designed for multi-room audio, standalone playback, or integration into a broader home cinema setup. According to Yamaha Australia, the unit delivers up to 40 watts of total output via a two-way driver configuration featuring a 9 cm woofer, a 3 cm soft dome tweeter, and dual passive radiators. For local streaming, it supports high-resolution audio formats including FLAC, WAV, and AIFF files up to 192 kHz / 24-bit, as well as ALAC up to 96 kHz / 24-bit. Connectivity options include dual-band Wi-Fi, Apple AirPlay 2, Bluetooth 4.2, and a physical Ethernet port.

## Who it suits
This speaker suits listeners invested in the Yamaha ecosystem who want flexible multi-room placement without running audio cables. Yamaha indicates the WX-021 can operate alone, be linked with a second unit for stereo playback, or serve as wireless rear surround speakers when paired with compatible MusicCast AV receivers or soundbars. It also suits smart homes running Apple Home via AirPlay 2 or setups managed with Home Assistant, which supports the speaker through an official integration.

However, buyers looking for a portable speaker should look elsewhere. The MusicCast 20 contains no internal battery and relies entirely on mains power. It also lacks a 3.5 mm auxiliary audio input, meaning all input sources must run over Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or Bluetooth. Furthermore, users seeking a standalone voice speaker should note that the WX-021 lacks built-in microphones; voice commands through Google Assistant or Amazon Alexa require a separate compatible smart device. Note also that physical volume and preset controls on the unit are disabled when configured as surround channels.

## Australian notes
Australian stock of the MusicCast 20 includes an Australian 240V mains power cord connecting to an internal AC power supply. Yamaha Australia lists operating power consumption at 10 watts, dropping to between 0.3 watts and 1.9 watts in standby modes.

Network connectivity accommodates both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi bands, which helps avoid congestion on older home routers, alongside an RJ-45 Ethernet port for wired reliability. The speaker does not require an ongoing subscription to access the Yamaha MusicCast Controller app. Local purchases through authorised Australian retailers come backed by a two-year manufacturer warranty from Yamaha Australia.
