The Philips Hue Lily Spotlight Base Kit (model 1741430P7) is a 24V DC low-voltage outdoor lighting set containing three matte black aluminium spotlights. Philips Hue states each 8W fixture outputs between 590 and 640 lumens at 4000K (350 lumens at 2700K) and offers White and Colour Ambiance spanning 16 million colours alongside adjustable colour temperatures from 2,000K to 6,500K. The fixtures carry an IP65 weather-resistance rating and connect via outdoor cabling to an included 40W power supply unit. Rather than operating over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, the Lily spotlights communicate using Zigbee mesh, requiring a separate Philips Hue Bridge for standard app and voice control.

## Who it suits

This kit suits households looking for decorative garden illumination, plant uplighting, or patio accent lighting. Because it operates through Zigbee, it is suited to homes that already maintain a Philips Hue setup or an independent Zigbee coordinator, such as Home Assistant. According to the manufacturer, automations like arrival lighting, schedules, and presence simulation operate via the Bridge without an ongoing subscription fee. It also fits users who want wide platform compatibility; the Hue Bridge provides integration with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, and Matter. However, buyers seeking standalone fixtures that connect directly to home Wi-Fi or Bluetooth without extra hardware will want to skip this kit. It is also designed for landscape accents rather than high-output perimeter security.

## Australian notes

The kit comes supplied with a 40W outdoor power supply unit built to plug directly into a standard Australian 220V–240V outdoor mains outlet. Because it operates on a 24V Safe Extra Low Voltage (SELV) line, the garden cables and fixtures do not require an electrician to install. However, if your outdoor space lacks a weatherproof mains socket, you should check your state's electrical safety regulator regarding mandatory requirements for outdoor mains outlet installation by a licensed electrical contractor. The bundled 40W power unit has sufficient capacity to run up to five 8W Lily fixtures; expanding past that requires replacing the supply with a higher-wattage unit. Philips Hue provides a two-year manufacturer warranty through local Australian retail channels.
