The Aqara Doorbell Camera Hub G410 (also catalogued as the Doorbell Camera G4 Matter) is a dual-power video doorbell bundled with an indoor chime repeater that doubles as a smart-home control centre. According to Aqara, the camera delivers 2K video clarity with a 175-degree field of view and a 4:3 aspect ratio, allowing users to keep watch over visitors and doorstep parcels. Unlike entry-level units relying exclusively on passive infrared sensors, Aqara states the device employs millimetre-wave (mmWave) radar alongside on-device facial recognition to track movement accurately without cloud dependence. The included indoor chime features a 95 dB speaker, local storage slots, and built-in radio hardware that acts as a Zigbee 3.0 hub, Thread border router, and Matter controller.

## Who it suits

This doorbell is primarily targeted at Apple Home users looking for battery or wired HomeKit Secure Video functionality without recurring subscription costs. Because Aqara supports Matter, Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Home Assistant, it also suits households running diverse device brands across automated smart locks and exterior lighting. Renters will appreciate the battery setup powered by six AA cells—rated by Aqara for up to five months of operation—combined with an adhesive mounting bracket that avoids permanent exterior alterations. Homeowners with existing doorbell wiring can hardwire the unit to unlock continuous 24/7 recording to an inserted microSD card or local network-attached storage (NAS). However, buyers with fully exposed front doors should reconsider: the exterior camera carries an IPX3 rating, which Aqara indicates requires sheltered placement away from driving rain.

## Australian notes

For Australian installations, the doorbell accepts standard low-voltage doorbell wiring supplying 12V to 24V AC or 8V to 24V DC, while the indoor chime hub is powered via a standard 5V USB-C input. If your home lacks a low-voltage circuit and requires a new transformer wired to 230V mains, check your state or territory's electrical safety regulator to verify whether a licensed electrician must carry out the work. Renters should consult their relevant state residential tenancy authority or lease agreement before drilling mounting screws into outdoor surfaces. Because wide-angle lenses can capture public footpaths or neighbouring properties, check your state or territory's surveillance and privacy laws regarding domestic camera placement. The chime hub connects to local dual-band 2.4GHz and 5GHz Wi-Fi networks, and retail units supplied by Australian stockists such as Officeworks and Bunnings include standard 12-month warranty protections alongside Australian Consumer Law coverage.
