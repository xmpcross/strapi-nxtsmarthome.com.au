The Sensibo Air is a compact smart climate controller that bridges the gap between an existing air conditioner and a connected smart home. According to Sensibo, the device replaces manual interaction with handheld infrared remotes by sending IR commands directly to your cooling or heating unit, including split systems, portable models, and window units. It features integrated ambient temperature and relative humidity sensors, allowing room conditions to trigger schedules and basic automation via the Sensibo companion app. Because it operates externally using line-of-sight infrared signals, the controller does not require any wiring into internal air conditioning circuitry.

## Who it suits
This controller suits homeowners and renters seeking to add remote scheduling, geofencing, and voice control to older or non-connected air conditioning units. It is particularly practical for multi-platform households, as Sensibo confirms out-of-the-box integration with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, SmartThings, and Home Assistant via its public API. Pet owners wanting to automate daytime cooling thresholds based on room temperature will also find utility in its free core automation tools.

However, users seeking native Matter support should skip this standard model or examine the Air PRO, as Sensibo confirms the standard Air lacks Matter compatibility. It also requires an unblocked line of sight within six metres of the air conditioner's receiver. Furthermore, because infrared communication is one-way, using your unit's original plastic remote alongside the Sensibo app can lead to out-of-sync status displays. Those wanting extended multi-month logs, anti-mould cycles, or open-window detection should also note that Sensibo locks these features behind an optional Sensibo Plus subscription.

## Australian notes
According to Australian retail specifications from Bunnings and JB Hi-Fi, local Sensibo Air units ship with a compliant 240V Australian mains power adapter and a micro-USB cable. Because it draws power from a standard 5V wall adapter rather than requiring hardwired mains electrical work, licensed electrician installation is not needed. For renters, the device can rest on a flat surface or be affixed using adhesive; check your state or territory residential tenancy authority regarding wall fixings.

Network connectivity is restricted to 2.4 GHz 802.11 b/g/n Wi-Fi bands, meaning Australian homes with combined or 5 GHz-only router bands may need to separate network frequencies during initial setup. Sensibo provides a voluntary 12-month manufacturer warranty in Australia, which sits alongside statutory consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law.
