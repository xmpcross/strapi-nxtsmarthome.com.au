The Govee Smart Colour Downlight (model R1501 / R1501EC1) is a ceiling-mounted light designed to fit standard 90 mm cut-outs. According to Australian retailer listings from Bunnings and Clever Gear, the fitting provides RGBWW lighting that covers 16 million colours and tunable white temperatures from 2700K warm white to 6500K cool daylight. The unit connects directly using 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, functioning without a proprietary bridge and offering Matter support for interoperability across major platforms.

## Who it suits

This downlight suits homeowners and renters with existing 90 mm ceiling holes who want flexible colour and white ambience linked to Apple Home, Google Home, or Samsung SmartThings. Govee's technical documentation confirms LAN API support, making it an option for Home Assistant users who prefer local network control over cloud dependencies. It also suits households already using Govee equipment who want to take advantage of Govee DreamView lighting synchronisation across the room.

It is not suitable for circuits controlled by traditional in-wall dimmer switches; retailer specifications note that dimming must be managed via app or voice controls. Because retailer and manufacturer documentation does not publish an IP rating, it should not be assumed suitable for wet areas like bathrooms or outdoor soffits. Buyers seeking specific illumination targets should also note that lumen ratings and wattage are not published.

## Australian notes

Retail listings from Bunnings and authorised distributor Clever Gear confirm the downlight comes equipped with an attached Australian mains plug lead. If your ceiling cavity features pre-wired surface sockets, the unit can simply be plugged in. However, if new socket outlets or wiring adjustments are needed, check your state's electrical safety regulator to verify whether a licensed electrician is required.

Local retailers including Scorptec and Clever Gear specify a one-year warranty for Australian purchases. Installation requires a standard 90 mm ceiling opening, so measure existing holes before ordering. For network connectivity, ensure your home router broadcasts a distinct 2.4 GHz band, as the light cannot connect to 5 GHz networks.
