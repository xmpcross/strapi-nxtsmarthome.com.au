The Google TV Streamer 4K (model GA05662-AU) is a dedicated set-top media player and smart home hub designed to replace older dongle-style Chromecast hardware. According to Google Store Australia, the device provides video output up to 4K HDR at 60 frames per second, supporting Dolby Vision, HDR10, HDR10+, and HLG formats, alongside Dolby Atmos audio pass-through. Google's published specifications report a 22 percent increase in CPU performance over the Chromecast with Google TV, paired with 4 GB of RAM, 32 GB of onboard storage, and a Gigabit Ethernet port for hardwired local network connections.

## Who it suits
This unit primarily suits households committed to Google Home and emerging Matter-based setups. Because Google specifies that the streamer operates as both a Matter controller and a Thread border router, it serves as a central hardware bridge for compatible smart devices without requiring a standalone hub. The on-screen Google TV Home Panel also allows users to monitor and adjust smart lighting, cameras, and sensors directly from the television interface.

Conversely, households that run Apple Home or Amazon Alexa setups will find little direct utility, as manufacturer documentation confirms no native platform support for either environment. Users seeking completely hands-free voice control without picking up a controller should also take note: Google confirms the streamer does not include far-field microphones on the base unit itself, meaning voice commands require pressing the microphone button on the remote or routing inputs through an existing Google Nest speaker. Additionally, buyers will need to supply their own HDMI 2.1 cable, as Google does not package one with the device.

## Australian notes
The Australian retail package (model GA05662-AU) includes an Australian AC wall adapter and a 1.8-metre USB-C power cable in the box, avoiding the need for aftermarket power adapters. For networking, the console supports dual-band 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac) networks alongside its physical Gigabit Ethernet connection.

Setting up the streamer requires a standard, free Google Account. Accessing streaming services such as Netflix, Stan, and Disney+ requires active paid subscriptions with each respective provider. Distributed locally through retailers including JB Hi-Fi, Harvey Norman, and Officeworks, the device includes a 12-month manufacturer warranty, which applies alongside statutory consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law.
