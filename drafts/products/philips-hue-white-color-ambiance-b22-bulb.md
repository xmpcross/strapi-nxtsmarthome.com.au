The Philips Hue White and Colour Ambiance A60 B22 smart bulb is an LED replacement globe designed for standard Australian bayonet fixtures. According to Philips Hue Australia, the current generation delivers up to 1,100 lumens (equivalent to a 75 W incandescent bulb) with an active power draw of 8.1 W. The manufacturer notes the bulb reproduces 16 million colours alongside tunable white temperatures spanning 1,000 K to 20,000 K, with ultra-low dimming down to 0.2% brightness. The unit communicates via Bluetooth and Zigbee rather than onboard Wi-Fi, allowing you to use it as an isolated single-room globe directly from your phone, or integrate it into a wider Zigbee network.

## Who it suits

This bulb suits renters and homeowners wanting adjustable colour and white tones in standard lamp bases or batten holders without replacing hardwired wall fittings. Renters will appreciate that it requires no wiring changes: you simply swap out the existing B22 globe. If you only want to control lighting in a bedroom or study, Philips Hue states you can pair up to 10 bulbs over direct Bluetooth to adjust colours and dimming via the app or voice using Amazon Alexa and Google Home. However, if you want whole-home scheduling, control while away from the house, or Apple Home integration, you will need to invest in a separate Philips Hue Bridge. Anyone looking for direct Wi-Fi smart bulbs that operate without an external hub may prefer looking elsewhere.

## Australian notes

In Australia, bayonet (B22) fittings are widely found across residential ceiling battens, pendant lights, and floor lamps. Because this bulb slots directly into an existing socket, it does not require electrical work or a licensed electrician. However, Philips Hue states that this globe cannot be dimmed via traditional wall-mounted dimmer dials; you must leave the wall switch on and dim via the app or Hue wireless accessories. If you have hardwired dimmer switches on the circuit, check your state's electrical safety regulator regarding rules for replacing hardwired switch plates. According to Australian retailer listings from Harvey Norman and Officeworks, the globe is backed by a 2-year manufacturer limited warranty.
