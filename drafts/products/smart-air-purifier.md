The Philips PureProtect 3200 Series Smart Air Purifier (model AC3220/10) is a dual-fan indoor air treatment appliance designed to circulate and clean air across expansive living spaces. Philips states that its dual-fan configuration produces a Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) of 520 m³/h, which the manufacturer rates to purify spaces of up to 135 m². Air purification is handled by a three-stage filtration setup: a pre-filter captures large dust particles and pet hair, a NanoProtect HEPA layer traps 99.97% of particles down to 0.003 microns, and an active carbon layer targets household odours and volatile organic compounds (VOCs). The unit includes built-in AeraSense sensors that monitor air quality constantly, displaying real-time particulate (PM2.5) levels through a colour indicator ring and reporting historical data to the Philips Air+ smartphone app.

## Who it suits

This purifier suits homeowners and apartment dwellers with spacious open-plan lounge, dining, or kitchen layouts who need rapid air circulation during bushfire smoke episodes, high-pollen seasons, or everyday cooking. Because Philips rates the quietest fan setting at 15 dB(A) using its SilentWings blade geometry, it also suits large master bedrooms or nurseries where low night-time sound output is essential. Users who already run Google Assistant or Amazon Alexa ecosystems can add the purifier to control fan speeds, adjust operating modes, or trigger voice commands. However, smart homes built around Apple Home will find no native HomeKit or Matter integration, requiring users to rely on the Philips Air+ app or third-party bridge platforms.

## Australian notes

The PureProtect 3200 Series is widely available from major Australian electrical retailers, including JB Hi-Fi, Harvey Norman, and Bing Lee. Units sold through official Australian channels feature a standard Australian 230V plug, carry Regulatory Compliance Mark (RCM) certification for local electrical safety standards, and are backed by a two-year Philips Australia warranty. Wireless control uses 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi; households with unified dual-band home routers may need to temporarily pause the 5 GHz band while completing initial setup. Renters should note that this floor-standing unit needs no wall mounting; just keep the power cord out of walkways. Ongoing maintenance requires changing the combined filter cartridge (model FY3200/30) approximately once a year, depending on runtime and local dust exposure.
