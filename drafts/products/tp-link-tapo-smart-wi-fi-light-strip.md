The TP-Link Tapo Smart Wi-Fi Light Strip (model Tapo L900-5) is a five-metre flexible LED strip designed for indoor accent lighting. According to TP-Link Australia, the strip provides 16 million RGB colours, dimming from 1% to 100%, and connects directly to home Wi-Fi networks without requiring a dedicated hardware hub. It runs via an included 12 V DC power adapter that plugs into standard Australian mains outlets and draws a maximum of 13.5 watts. TP-Link specifies a total luminous intensity of 2,100 mcd (approximately 400 lumens across the full run) and an estimated operational lifespan of up to 50,000 hours. The strip includes 3M adhesive backing and cut lines for fitting to specific furniture or architectural recesses.

## Who it suits

According to official specifications, this model suits users seeking mood or ambient backlighting behind televisions, desks, bedheads, or under cabinetry. Because it is powered from a standard plug rather than hardwired circuits, it suits renters looking for non-permanent lighting options. However, buyers should note its output limitations: with roughly 400 lumens across five metres, TP-Link positions this as decorative accent lighting rather than functional task illumination. It will not suit users wanting segmented, chasing, or multi-colour rainbow effects simultaneously, as the L900 series is a single-zone strip that displays one uniform colour at a time. Users committed to an Apple Home setup should also note that native HomeKit is omitted, though TP-Link notes Siri Shortcuts can be triggered through the Tapo application.

## Australian notes

Retail units sourced through Australian stockists include a 220–240 V AC adapter fitted with a standard Australian plug. It does not involve fixed wiring, but if you intend to add new recessed power points to conceal cables, check your state's electrical safety regulator regarding mandatory licencing rules. Connectivity is restricted to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n), so your router must broadcast an active 2.4 GHz network band during pairing. TP-Link Australia provides a two-year warranty for the product, which sits alongside statutory consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law. The Tapo app provides all scheduling, timers, and lighting modes without a paid subscription.
