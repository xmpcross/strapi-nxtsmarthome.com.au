The Amazon Echo Dot (5th Gen) is a compact smart speaker measuring 100 mm by 100 mm by 89 mm and weighing approximately 304 grams. It houses a 44 mm front-firing speaker driver designed for voice responses, audiobooks, and music streaming. Powered natively by Amazon’s Alexa platform, the hardware features physical push buttons for volume adjustment, action triggers, and a dedicated microphone-off privacy mute, along with top-tap gesture controls to pause audio or silence timers. It connects using Bluetooth and dual-band Wi-Fi, drawing power continuously via an external 15W DC mains adapter.

## Who it suits

This device suits Australian renters and homeowners invested in the Amazon Alexa ecosystem who want room-level voice control without installing separate hardware sensors. With integrated ultrasound motion detection and an ambient temperature sensor, the speaker allows users to build automation routines in the Alexa app, such as turning on lights when presence is detected or activating cooling fans when a room warms up. It also suits homes operating eero mesh networks, as retailer specifications confirm it can act as a Wi-Fi extender for compatible eero setups.

Conversely, it is not suited to households built strictly around Apple Home or Google Home, as neither platform is natively supported. While it operates as a Matter controller over Wi-Fi, it does not include Thread border router capabilities or an onboard Zigbee radio. Buyers looking to route sound through existing external stereo equipment should also take note: PCMag Australia confirms Amazon removed the 3.5 mm auxiliary audio port found on earlier generations.

## Australian notes

The Echo Dot (5th Gen) is stocked across Australian retailers, including JB Hi-Fi, The Good Guys, Officeworks, BIG W, and Mobileciti, and carries a 12-month manufacturer warranty. 

Connecting to local home networks is handled over dual-band 802.11a/b/g/n/ac Wi-Fi across both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz channels. The unit must stay connected to a standard power point using its supplied 15W (12V / 1.25A) external power adapter. Core smart assistant functions, device controls, and routines require only a free Amazon account, although streaming through services such as Spotify Premium, Apple Music, and Amazon Music Unlimited requires separate paid subscriptions.
