The Go Smart Bridge (model C-BR300), developed in partnership with Neo Smart Blinds, is an automation hub designed to control motorised window coverings. According to manufacturer specifications from Go Smart Blinds and Neo Smart Blinds, the unit translates smart home commands into 433.92 MHz radio frequency (RF) signals to automate compatible blinds, shades, and curtains. It connects to home networks using 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and provides app-based automation for up to 30 rooms without requiring a paid subscription.

## Who it suits

This bridge suits households that already have compatible 433.92 MHz motorised blinds and want to connect them to broader platforms. Neo Smart Blinds and Go Smart Blinds confirm that C-BR300 units with serial numbers starting with "SR" running firmware 0.2 or higher support Matter. This allows bridging into Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Samsung SmartThings. It also suits smart-home enthusiasts running Home Assistant, as the manufacturer notes support via a custom component.

Buyers should skip this device if their window coverings rely on incompatible proprietary protocols. Go Smart Blinds specifies that the hub does not work with remotes or motors from Lutron, Hunter Douglas, Rollease ARC/AMP, or E-Series systems. It is also unsuitable for anyone expecting an off-the-shelf local hardware kit with Australian plugs.

## Australian notes

Go Smart Blinds does not maintain an Australian distribution presence or a regional manufacturer website. Units in Australia are sourced through cross-border retailers such as Ubuy, eBay, or Amazon AU. Consequently, the manufacturer's one-year limited warranty operates out of North America, leaving Australian purchasers reliant on the return policies of the importing seller.

For power, the bridge requires a 5V DC / 1A supply. Manufacturer listings confirm that the included power adapter uses a North American plug, meaning Australian households must supply their own standard Type I USB wall adapter. Network setup is strictly limited to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n), with 5 GHz connections unsupported. Neo Smart Blinds documentation notes that dual-band routers using band steering or "Smart Connect" may require temporary adjustment during initial pairing to ensure successful connection.
