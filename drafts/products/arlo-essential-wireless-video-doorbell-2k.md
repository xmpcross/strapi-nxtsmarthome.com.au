The Arlo Essential Wireless Video Doorbell 2K (model AVD4001-100AUS) is a connected entrance camera designed to monitor visitors and deliveries at your front door. According to Arlo's Australian specifications, the unit captures video at 2K HDR resolution with night vision and employs a 180-degree diagonal field of view. Its 1:1 square aspect ratio allows users to see approaching callers from head to toe as well as parcels placed flat on the ground. When a visitor presses the button, the doorbell dials the Arlo Secure app as a direct video call. Users can communicate using two-way audio, play pre-recorded quick responses, or sound an integrated siren to ward off intruders.

## Who it suits
This doorbell suits households and renters looking for an entrance camera that functions wire-free without requiring a dedicated base station. Because it connects directly to standard Wi-Fi, it simplifies initial deployment. For renters, wire-free battery installation avoids cutting or pulling cables, though fixing anything to exterior surfaces is worth checking against your lease and your state or territory tenancy authority first. Homes running Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant will appreciate that live streams can route to compatible smart displays.

Those wanting local storage or zero recurring fees should look at alternatives. Arlo states that while live video and basic ring notifications work without a subscription, video clip history, 30-day cloud recording, and smart alerts that differentiate between people, vehicles, and packages require an ongoing paid Arlo Secure subscription. In addition, Apple HomeKit and Matter users will find no native support. Because the rechargeable battery is sealed inside the device, recharging via USB takes the doorbell out of commission while plugged in indoors.

## Australian notes
The doorbell connects exclusively over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, meaning strong coverage from your router to the front entryway is necessary. It comes with a 12-month manufacturer warranty through major Australian retailers including JB Hi-Fi, Officeworks, and Bunnings.

While designed to run wire-free on battery, Arlo confirms the device can also connect to existing low-voltage doorbell wiring between 8V AC and 24V AC for continuous battery trickle charging. If you plan to wire the unit into an existing mechanical chime or install a new doorbell transformer, check regulations with your state or territory's electrical safety regulator. Lastly, if your front entrance faces a footpath, apartment common area, or neighbouring property, check local state surveillance and privacy legislation regarding outdoor recording devices.
