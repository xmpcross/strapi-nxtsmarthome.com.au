The Ring Alarm 5-Piece Security Kit (2nd Gen) is a wireless DIY security system designed for apartments and smaller houses. The kit includes an Alarm Base Station with a built-in 104-decibel siren and battery backup, a second-generation Keypad, one door or window Contact Sensor, one motion detector, and a Range Extender. The peripherals connect to the Base Station over Z-Wave wireless protocol, and the hub links to your home network using either 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi or a wired Ethernet cable. Everyday control is handled via the physical keypad or the Ring mobile application.

## Who it suits
This system suits renters and apartment owners who want a modular security system without drilling holes or altering building infrastructure. Because the contact sensor and motion detector mount using supplied adhesive strips or lightweight brackets, they can be removed cleanly when moving; confirm any mounting rules in your lease agreement or with your state residential tenancy tribunal before installation.

It also suits existing users of Amazon Alexa and Ring video doorbells or security cameras. Through the Ring app, users can create automations that trigger camera recordings when an alarm sensor trips, while Alexa speakers can announce sensor status, chime on door events, and accept voice pin codes for arming.

However, larger properties will quickly outgrow this starter set, as a single contact sensor and one motion detector leave most perimeter doors and ground-floor windows unguarded. Users invested in Apple Home, Google Home, or Matter ecosystems should look elsewhere, as Ring does not offer cross-platform interoperability for these environments.

## Australian notes
Prospective Australian buyers need to be aware of service limitations. Ring officially restricts its 24/7 Professional Monitoring and emergency police dispatch to the United States and Canada. In Australia, the kit operates strictly as a self-monitored alarm system that relies on push notifications and the hub's audible siren. Furthermore, Ring requires an active Ring subscription to access remote app controls, such as arming and disarming via your phone.

Hardware provenance also matters. Ring primarily supplies this kit under North American specifications (model 4K11SZ-0EN0). While the Base Station's AC adapter accepts universal 100–240V inputs, it requires an Australian plug adapter, and the included plug-in Range Extender is rated for 120V US outlets. The internal cellular backup module is programmed for North American LTE networks and may not establish a cellular connection in Australia. Additionally, imported hardware communicates using North American Z-Wave frequencies (902–928 MHz) rather than the Australian standard (921.4 MHz), meaning you cannot expand the kit with locally purchased Australian Z-Wave accessories.
