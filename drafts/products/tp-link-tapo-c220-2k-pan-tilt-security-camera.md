The TP-Link Tapo C220 is a mains-powered indoor security camera featuring motorised pan-and-tilt adjustment. According to TP-Link Australia, the unit uses a 4-megapixel sensor recording in 2K QHD resolution (2560 × 1440), offering 360-degree horizontal rotation and 114-degree vertical tilt. The camera includes 850 nm infrared night vision rated up to 9 metres, two-way audio through an integrated microphone and speaker, and on-device AI detection for people, pets, vehicles, and unusual noises like glass breaking or baby crying. Footage can be saved locally to a microSD card up to 512 GB or backed up via an optional paid Tapo Care cloud subscription.

## Who it suits
This camera suits Australian households looking to monitor living areas, hallways, nurseries, or indoor pets without committing to ongoing monthly cloud fees. Because TP-Link provides person and pet identification on the hardware itself alongside local microSD storage, users can receive filtered notifications and store footage subscription-free. It is practical for renters seeking a non-permanent security device that can sit upright on a shelf or cabinet. For smart-home users building mixed environments, RTSP and ONVIF support allows the camera to stream to third-party software like Home Assistant or network video recorders. However, it should be skipped by anyone needing outdoor coverage, as it lacks weatherproofing, as well as those invested exclusively in Apple Home or Matter ecosystems.

## Australian notes
The Tapo C220 is sold in Australia with an Australian-standard AC power adapter providing 9.0 V DC power over a 3-metre cable. The unit does not contain a backup battery, so it will turn off during power cuts. Network connectivity relies entirely on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11b/g/n), meaning your router must broadcast on the 2.4 GHz band, and there is no wired Ethernet port for local network cabling. Platform support includes Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Samsung SmartThings. TP-Link covers the camera with a 3-year manufacturer warranty under its Australian warranty policy. If you position this camera near windows or shared entries, check your state or territory surveillance devices legislation regarding indoor audio and video recording.
