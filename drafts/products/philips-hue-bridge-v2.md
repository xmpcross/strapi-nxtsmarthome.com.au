The Philips Hue Bridge (V2) is a dedicated smart lighting hub that connects Philips Hue Zigbee lights and accessories to your local network. According to Philips Hue Australia, the compact unit measures 88 mm × 88 mm × 26 mm and uses the 2.4 GHz Zigbee frequency band to manage up to 50 lights and 12 accessories. Rather than communicating over home Wi-Fi, the bridge connects directly to your router using a wired RJ-45 Ethernet port. It also operates as a Matter bridge through firmware updates, allowing paired Hue devices to surface across major platforms including Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, and Home Assistant.

## Who it suits

This bridge suits homeowners and renters building out a Philips Hue lighting ecosystem who need multi-room scheduling, automations, and broad ecosystem compatibility. Because it functions as a Matter bridge, it is practical for homes running cross-platform setups or multi-assistant households where users want Hue devices shared across Apple Home and Google Home without third-party workarounds. It also suits Home Assistant users seeking native local integration. However, households planning for more than 50 lights will need multiple hubs, as Philips Hue notes capacity is capped per unit. Those looking for an all-in-one hub with Thread Border Router capabilities or flexible wireless placement should skip it, as this unit lacks Thread radios and requires an Ethernet port near your router.

## Australian notes

Retailers including JB Hi-Fi, Bunnings, and Officeworks stock the Hue Bridge V2 in Australia with a 2-year local warranty. The device includes a plug-pack power supply rated for 100–240 V AC at 50–60 Hz, matching standard Australian 230 V mains power. Because the unit plugs into a standard power outlet and connects via an RJ-45 cable, no electrical wiring or licensed trade work is involved. Renters can deploy it without modifying fixtures or wall plates. For networking, note that the bridge contains no internal Wi-Fi hardware. You will need a spare LAN port on your modem, router, or network switch, alongside an available power point. Philips Hue Australia states that standard operation, remote access, and automations require only a free Hue account, with no ongoing subscription fees for standard lighting control.
