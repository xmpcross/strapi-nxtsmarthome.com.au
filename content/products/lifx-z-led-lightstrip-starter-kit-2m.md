The LIFX Z LED Lightstrip Starter Kit (model LZ3SK2MAU) is a mains-powered indoor accent lighting solution comprising two 1-metre strips, an inline controller, and an Australian power supply. According to LIFX, the lightstrip delivers up to 1,400 lumens across its 2-metre length (700 lumens per metre) and incorporates Polychrome Technology. This allows for 8 addressable colour zones per metre—providing 16 distinct zones out of the box—which can display multiple colours simultaneously or animate via the LIFX app without requiring a dedicated hardware hub.

## Who it suits
This kit suits homeowners and renters seeking accent lighting for television backlighting, under-cabinet joinery, or shelving. Because it uses adhesive backing and plugs into a standard power point, it allows renters to add dynamic lighting without altering household wiring. For tenants planning to stick the strip onto painted walls or rental cabinetry, check your tenancy agreement regarding surface adhesives and potential paint peel before installation.

It also suits multi-platform households. LIFX notes direct compatibility with Apple Home, Amazon Alexa, Google Home, Samsung SmartThings, and direct local LAN control via Home Assistant. If your setup relies on cross-platform automations, this wide ecosystem support simplifies integration.

However, buyers requiring outdoor illumination should skip this kit, as LIFX specifies it is strictly for indoor use with no water resistance rating. It is also unsuitable for tight, sharp corner folds across the LED modules; LIFX notes the strip only tolerates gentle curves up to 90 degrees, meaning complex cabinetry runs may require specialised third-party corner connectors.

## Australian notes
LIFX packages this starter kit with a standard Australian power supply unit designed for local 230V, 50Hz mains power. The unit includes a 0.5-metre lead from the wall plug to the inline controller and a 2.0-metre lead connecting the controller to the lightstrip, which provides flexibility when positioning near wall sockets.

Network connectivity relies entirely on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11b/g/n); it does not support 5 GHz networks. If you use a dual-band Australian mesh router, you may need to ensure your phone connects to the 2.4 GHz band during initial pairing. LIFX backs the product with a 2-year limited manufacturer warranty in Australia. Additionally, while LIFX has rolled out Matter support via firmware updates, manufacturer support documentation notes this applies only to hardware revisions containing the newer LCM3 microcontroller.
