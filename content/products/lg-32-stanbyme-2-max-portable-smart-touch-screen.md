The LG StanbyME 2 Max (model 32LX6BDGA) is a portable 32-inch 4K UHD smart display designed to move between living areas, home offices, and kitchens. According to LG Australia, the unit sits on a movable wheeled docking stand capable of height adjustments, tilt, swivel, and portrait-to-landscape rotation, while also detaching to sit on an integrated kickstand or an optional wall mount. The panel runs webOS 26 powered by the Alpha 8 AI Processor 4K Gen3, incorporating edge-lit LED backlighting alongside Dolby Vision, HDR10, and HLG playback. Audio is delivered via built-in 2.0-channel 10W side-firing speakers with Dolby Atmos. An integrated lithium battery supports cord-free playback, which LG notes can reach up to 4.5 hours in Eco mode before needing recharge via USB-C or AC power.

## Who it suits
This portable display suits Australian renters and homeowners seeking a mobile television and smart dashboard that avoids permanent wall mounting. Because the stand rolls on wheels and detaches onto a built-in kickstand, it works well for users following recipes in the kitchen, joining video calls, or viewing media throughout the home. LG confirms the webOS Home Hub supports Matter, Apple Home via AirPlay 2, Google Home via Google Cast, and Amazon Alexa.

However, users wanting a dedicated high-end gaming monitor should look elsewhere; retailer specifications confirm the display is capped at a 60Hz refresh rate without high-frame-rate modes. LG also notes that touch interface support varies by application, meaning certain third-party streaming apps require the included remote rather than screen taps. Additionally, the USB-C ports do not support wired USB headphones, and LG specifies that accessing AI Acoustic Tuning requires purchasing an LG AI Magic Remote separately.

## Australian notes
In Australia, the 32LX6BDGA is backed by a standard 12-month manufacturer warranty from LG Australia. The unit operates on a 100–240V 50/60Hz power supply using an included detachable power cable, and can also recharge using compatible USB-C Power Delivery gear. Connectivity covers Wi-Fi 6E across 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz bands, ensuring compatibility with standard Australian home wireless routers. While free streaming channels are included via LG Channels, local and international platforms like Stan, Netflix, and Disney+ require their own separate paid subscriptions.
