The Dreame X40 Ultra (model RLX63CE) is a dual-function robot vacuum and mop paired with an automated multi-function base station. Dreame positions the unit for thorough floor maintenance, equipping it with 12,000 Pa suction and rotating mop pads that extend outward to clean along skirting boards and around furniture legs. When transitioning across carpets, the robotic vacuum mechanically raises both its mopping pads by 10.5 mm and its side brush by 10 mm to prevent damp residue from transferring onto fibres. At the base station, the unit automatically transfers collected dust into a 3.2-litre sealed bag, refills its internal water tank, washes mop pads with 70°C heated water, and dries them with warm air.

## Who it suits
This robot cleaner is designed for larger households with mixed flooring, including tiled living zones, timber floorboards, and low-to-medium pile carpets. Dreame claims its OmniDirt detection system identifies sticky floor grime via an onboard RGB camera and optical turbidity sensors, instructing the unit to re-wash its pads and run repeat mopping passes where needed. Pet owners will find the pet-recognition mode and obstacle avoidance useful, as Dreame reports the front-facing 3D structured light and camera navigate around scattered pet toys, footwear, and cables without jamming. However, households dominated by thick, high-pile carpets may find the 10.5 mm lifting clearance tight, and homes with tight floor layouts may struggle to accommodate the dock's footprint.

## Australian notes
Units sourced through authorised Australian retailers come equipped with a standard Australian plug suited to 230V mains power and carry Regulatory Compliance Mark (RCM) certification. Australian stock commonly includes a 36-month manufacturer warranty. For wireless setup, the vacuum operates exclusively on 2.4GHz Wi-Fi frequencies; routers combining 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands into a single SSID may require band splitting or a dedicated 2.4GHz network during initial Dreamehome app pairing. Voice integration works with Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa. Because the unit includes a front-facing RGB camera that supports live remote video surveillance, occupants in rental accommodation or shared households should check relevant state surveillance device legislation regarding camera use in private premises.
