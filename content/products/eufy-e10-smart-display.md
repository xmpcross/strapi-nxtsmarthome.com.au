The Eufy All in One Smart Display E10 (model T87A0) is an 8-inch indoor monitoring screen designed to operate as a central hub for Eufy security devices. Eufy Australia notes the unit features an IPS LCD touchscreen with 1280 × 800 resolution, a 2.5W speaker, and a 4-microphone array for two-way audio. It can show up to four live camera streams simultaneously. Rather than acting as a standard multimedia tablet, it is built specifically to cache security events locally onto 8 GB of internal storage or an optional microSD card up to 512 GB, without requiring a monthly cloud subscription.\n\n## Who it suits\n\nThis unit suits Australian homeowners and renters who have already invested in Eufy security cameras, video doorbells, or smart locks and want an always-ready console instead of relying entirely on phone notifications. Because it includes a 4,050 mAh lithium battery alongside a magnetic charging dock and wall-mounting bracket, users can move the display between rooms or mount it by an entrance. If you want to answer your Eufy doorbell without opening a smartphone app, Eufy indicates the unit delivers chimes and camera feeds directly to the screen.\n\nConversely, households wanting an open smart-home screen should skip this product. It does not run Apple Home, Google Assistant, Amazon Alexa, or Matter, nor does it support music streaming or general smart-home automations. Furthermore, Eufy notes that advanced features like facial recognition and package detection depend on a connected Eufy HomeBase, such as the HomeBase S380.\n\n## Australian notes\n\nLocal retail listings through Harvey Norman, Bunnings Australia, and The Good Guys confirm the display ships with an Australian RCM-compliant 5V/2A power adapter and USB-C cable. Australian retailer listings show differing warranty lengths: The Good Guys lists a 1-year manufacturer warranty, whereas Bunnings Australia and CyberShack cite a 3-year manufacturer warranty, so verify coverage terms at your point of purchase.\n\nThe E10 supports dual-band 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) networks. If you plan to wall-mount the display in a rental property, note that drilling into plaster or running permanent wiring may require landlord consent; check your state or territory tenancy authority guidelines before fixing mounts to walls.
