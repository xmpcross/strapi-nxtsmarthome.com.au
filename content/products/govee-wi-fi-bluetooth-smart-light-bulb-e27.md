The Govee Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Smart Light Bulb E27 is a colour-changing LED globe designed for indoor lighting fixtures. Distributed in Australia by Clever Gear and sold across retailers including JB Hi-Fi, Bunnings, and Harvey Norman, the globe provides tunable white colour temperatures from 2700K to 6500K alongside RGBWW multicolour output. Retailer listings state the bulb delivers a typical output of 800 lumens with a rated power draw of 9 watts, operating over local 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connections without requiring a paid subscription.

## Who it suits

This globe suits Australian homeowners and renters looking to introduce schedule-based automation, scene presets, and voice control into standard screw-in lamps and light fixtures. Because it fits directly into an existing E27 lamp socket, renters can use it in portable floor or table lamps without altering fixed fittings. Check your local tenancy agreement before replacing landlord-supplied globes in fixed fittings. Users who want cross-platform ecosystem flexibility will find value in the H6004E01 variant, which supports Matter, allowing native setup within Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa.

However, it is not suited for households that rely on traditional in-wall dimmer switches. Retailers state that dimming is managed entirely through software or voice assistants, and running the globe on an in-wall dimmer circuit can cause operational faults. It should also be skipped if your home network is restricted strictly to 5 GHz Wi-Fi bands, or if you require native out-of-the-box Home Assistant integration, as neither the manufacturer nor local retailers list official platform support.

## Australian notes

In Australia, the bulb is rated for standard AC 220–240V mains power at 50/60 Hz under EESS approval number E2024150593. It features an E27 Edison screw base, which is widely used in Australian freestanding lamps and contemporary pendants. Buyers should check their fittings to confirm whether an E27 screw or a B22 bayonet cap is required before purchasing.

Shoppers should also pay attention to model numbers. Retail listings confirm two distinct variants sold in Australia: the H6004E01, which features Matter compatibility, and the standard H6004EC2, which relies solely on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and does not work with Matter or Apple Home. Both variants require a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi connection for pairing, and Australian stock is backed by a 12-month manufacturer warranty handled through authorised local retailers.
