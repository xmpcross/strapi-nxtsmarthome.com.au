The Swann Max+ 4K Ultra HD Security System listing corresponds to Swann's long-range wireless equipment, marketed in Australia as the MaxRanger4K solar security system. According to Swann Australia, the system connects wire-free outdoor cameras to an indoor hub using sub-1GHz Wi-Fi HaLow on the 900 MHz band, delivering up to 200 metres of typical wireless coverage. The cameras feature 4K Ultra HD video with Sony STARVIS sensors, integrated top-mounted solar panels, IP66 weatherproofing, and Night2Day colour night vision. Video recordings, spotlights, sirens, and two-way audio are managed directly through the Swann Security app, with motion and heat-triggered clips saved locally to an included 64 GB microSD card on the base hub without compulsory subscription fees.

## Who it suits
According to the manufacturer specifications, this system suits owners of large residential properties, rural blocks, or farms who need security monitoring across distances where standard 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi signals degrade. The wire-free design with built-in solar charging suits locations without exterior electrical wiring, provided the cameras receive adequate sunlight. Households looking to avoid ongoing cloud storage fees will appreciate the out-of-the-box local storage on the hub.

Conversely, it does not suit households seeking continuous 24/7 video recording, as Swann Support notes the system records motion-triggered events only to conserve battery power. Smart-home enthusiasts should also look elsewhere; Swann Support confirms there is no integration with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Home Assistant, or Matter. If you rely on voice commands to view camera feeds on smart displays or run cross-device automations, this system will not integrate with your setup.

## Australian notes
Swann confirms the hub connects to Australian mains power using an included standard Australian plug, while linking to your home network via a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi connection or an RJ45 Ethernet cable. The camera-to-hub transmission uses the 900 MHz spectrum under Australian radio communications arrangements for Wi-Fi HaLow.

Swann provides a standard 12-month manufacturer warranty in Australia, with options to extend coverage upon product registration. If you are positioning outdoor cameras facing boundary fences, shared driveways, or public footpaths, consult your state or territory surveillance and privacy laws to ensure recording compliance.
