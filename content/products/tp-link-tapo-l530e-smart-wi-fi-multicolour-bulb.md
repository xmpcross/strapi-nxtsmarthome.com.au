The TP-Link Tapo L530E is an A60-format smart LED light bulb fitted with an E27 Edison screw base. Rated at an output of 806 lumens (the equivalent of a traditional 60 W incandescent bulb) with an approximate power draw of 8.3 W to 8.7 W, the bulb connects directly to home Wi-Fi without needing a separate smart bridge or hub. According to TP-Link Australia, the unit provides 16 million colours alongside an adjustable white colour temperature spectrum ranging from 2,500 K warm white to 6,500 K cool daylight. Operating features such as scheduling, timers, Away Mode, and dimming from 1% to 100% are managed via the free Tapo mobile app.

## Who it suits

This bulb suits homeowners and renters seeking colour-adjustable ambient lighting for standard E27 light fittings. Because it screws directly into standard lamp bases and fixtures without physical alterations or electrical wiring, it offers renters a portable smart lighting option. It also suits smart homes built around Amazon Alexa or Google Home, both of which support direct voice and routine control through native integrations. Users managing Samsung SmartThings or running local Home Assistant systems can also connect the bulb, provided third-party compatibility is enabled in the Tapo settings.

Conversely, those who have standardised on Apple Home or require Matter compatibility should pass on the L530E, as TP-Link’s specifications confirm it does not support either standard. Households relying on traditional physical wall dimmers should also look elsewhere; according to TP-Link, the bulb cannot be used with physical in-wall dimmer switches and must remain powered at the wall switch to retain smart connectivity.

## Australian notes

The Tapo L530E sold by Australian retailers such as JB Hi-Fi, Officeworks, and The Good Guys is configured for Australian mains power, rated for 220–240 V AC at 50/60 Hz. The bulb uses a standard E27 Edison screw base; households with bayonet (B22) fixtures will need an adapter or an alternative model. Network connectivity is strictly limited to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11b/g/n), meaning routers with band steering may require a dedicated 2.4 GHz SSID during setup. TP-Link Australia provides a two-year manufacturer warranty on the device. If you are modifying in-wall light switches to control smart lighting circuits, check your state's electrical safety regulator to ensure licensed trade compliance.
