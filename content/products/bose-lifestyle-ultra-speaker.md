The Bose Lifestyle Ultra Speaker is a compact, mains-powered wireless speaker designed for home music playback, multi-room streaming, and home cinema expansion. According to Bose specifications published by Australian retailers like JB Hi-Fi and The Good Guys, the enclosure houses three multidirectional drivers—two front-facing drivers and one up-firing driver—operating with TrueSpatial audio processing and CleanBass technology. Measuring 184.7 mm high, 121.1 mm wide, and 167.5 mm deep, with a weight of 1.66 kg, the unit fits on shelves, desks, or bedside tables. Streaming options include dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.3, Apple AirPlay, Google Cast, and Spotify Connect. A physical 3.5 mm auxiliary analogue socket on the rear panel also allows owners to connect wired sources, such as a turntable with a built-in pre-amp, without additional network hardware.

## Who it suits
This model suits listeners seeking a versatile bookshelf speaker that handles diverse casting methods across both Apple and Android smartphones. Households that already use the Amazon Alexa ecosystem can take direct advantage of hands-free voice commands via built-in microphones, alongside the physical touch pad and the Bose mobile application. It also appeals to home theatre owners who want incremental upgrades: Bose confirms that a single unit works on its own, two can pair for dedicated two-channel stereo, or two units can serve as rear surround channels behind a compatible Bose Lifestyle Ultra Soundbar, where the up-firing drivers handle height elements. However, buyers wanting a portable speaker for outdoor patios should look elsewhere, as the unit relies strictly on mains electricity. Those heavily invested in voice-activated Google Assistant control should also note that onboard voice recognition is restricted to Alexa.

## Australian notes
Australian retail packages (model 894001-5110 for black and 894001-5210 for white smoke) ship with a standard Australian 240V AC power cable. The speaker meets Australian radio communications and electrical equipment standards, marked by the Regulatory Compliance Mark (RCM). Bose Australia provides a standard 12-month manufacturer warranty, which sits alongside guarantees provided under Australian Consumer Law. Operating the speaker's networked features requires a connection to a local 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz Wi-Fi router, and the initial setup, EQ adjustments, and firmware updates must be completed through the Bose mobile app.
