The Tapo T315 is a small battery-powered room sensor from TP-Link that measures temperature and humidity and shows both on a 2.7-inch e-ink screen. TP-Link Australia says the display also shows a comfort indicator (a simple face you can customise in the Tapo app), battery level and signal strength, and a side button switches between Celsius and Fahrenheit. The brand quotes accuracy of ±0.3°C and ±3% RH from a Swiss-made sensor, with readings refreshed every two seconds.

## Who it suits, and who should skip it

The key point is the hub. TP-Link states that a Tapo hub is required for the smart features: remote monitoring, home automation, notifications and data export. Without one, the T315 is essentially a well-specified desk hygrometer. If you already own Tapo smart plugs or a Tapo hub, it fits that ecosystem; TP-Link describes automations that switch heaters, fans or humidifiers when a room drifts outside a range you set. That makes it relevant to Australian bathrooms prone to mould, bedrooms on hot summer nights, or a nursery. If you are committed to Zigbee, Z-Wave or Wi-Fi sensors with no Tapo gear, a sensor native to your own hub will be simpler.

On ecosystems, TP-Link's support knowledge base lists the T315 among the sub-GHz sensors that its H100, H110, H200 and H500 hubs can expose through Matter bridging, which the article says makes the sensor visible to platforms such as Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings and Home Assistant. That route depends on the hub, not on the sensor itself, and the article is written for TP-Link's global community rather than Australia specifically.

## Australian notes and running costs

TP-Link Australia lists the radio as 922 MHz and the power source as two AAA (LR03) batteries, which are included in the box along with 3M adhesive. The brand claims up to two years of battery life, and notes this is based on its own laboratory results under normal use. Cloud storage of up to two years of data and CSV export are described as free, so beyond batteries and the hub there is no subscription mentioned by TP-Link. The Good Guys lists a one-year manufacturer's warranty. We saw the T315 listed at JB Hi-Fi, The Good Guys, Bunnings and Amazon AU.

Alternatives in our catalogue: the Aqara Temperature and Humidity Sensor T1 suits people building around Aqara's own hub and app. The ecobee SmartSensor 2-Pack is aimed at households with an ecobee thermostat. The Tuya Wifi Temperature Humidity Sensor is a Wi-Fi sensor for the Tuya Smart Life app, for those who would rather not add a Tapo hub.
