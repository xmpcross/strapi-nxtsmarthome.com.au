The Kogan SmarterHome Smart Touch Single Light Switch (model KASMSWTH1GA) is an in-wall replacement switch designed for a single lighting circuit. According to Kogan, it features a tempered glass front touch panel set in a polycarbonate frame, measuring 116 mm × 70 mm × 35 mm to fit standard single-gang wall plates. The switch connects directly to your home network using 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi—with Bluetooth onboard for setup—and operates via the Kogan SmarterHome app without requiring a paid subscription. It provides basic on-and-off toggling and scheduling, and Kogan notes that the built-in blue LED indicator light can be turned off in the app if installed in a bedroom. It handles a maximum load of 800W per gang, with specific limits of 300W for incandescent lighting and 150W for fluorescent loads.

## Who it suits

This unit suits homeowners who want straightforward on/off scheduling and voice switching via Google Home or Amazon Alexa. It is ideal for single-circuit light fixtures where dimming is not required. However, households invested in Apple Home, Matter, or SmartThings ecosystems should look elsewhere, as Kogan does not list integration for these platforms. Renters should also skip this switch, as it involves permanent wiring modifications to the wall cavity that are generally unsuitable for leased properties. Finally, anyone seeking multi-way switching or light-dimming features will need an alternative, as this unit is an on/off single-gang switch only.

## Australian notes

This product operates on standard Australian mains power (AC 100–240V, 50/60Hz). The manufacturer's user guide states that it must be protected by a 10A circuit breaker, cannot be used in SELV/PELV circuits, and strictly requires a neutral wire to function. Many older Australian properties do not have neutral wires run to wall switch plates, so your wiring configuration must be checked beforehand.

Because this is a mains-voltage hardwired device, installation is generally work for a licensed electrician; check your state's electrical safety regulator. Kogan provides a 1-year manufacturer warranty for Australian purchases.
