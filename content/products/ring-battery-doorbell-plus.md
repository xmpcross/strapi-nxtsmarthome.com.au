The Ring Battery Doorbell Plus is a wireless, battery-powered video doorbell designed for front-door monitoring without mains wiring. According to Ring, the camera features 1536p HD+ resolution with a 1:1 aspect ratio and an expanded 150-degree horizontal and vertical field of view, providing a head-to-toe perspective intended to capture standing visitors as well as parcels left on the doorstep. The unit includes colour night vision, two-way audio with noise cancellation, and Ring's Quick Replies feature for automated visitor messaging. It runs on a removable quick-release battery pack that recharges via USB, allowing you to recharge the battery without unscrewing the mounting plate.

## Who it suits
This doorbell is well suited to Amazon Alexa households. When paired with Alexa, Ring reports that compatible Echo smart speakers can announce visitor rings, while Echo Show displays can automatically stream the live front-door camera feed. It is also suitable for renters and homeowners looking for a DIY installation that avoids drilling into external walls or altering electrical circuits, particularly when paired with compatible no-drill adhesive mounts.

Conversely, households built around Apple Home or Google Home should look elsewhere, as Ring does not offer native support for either ecosystem or the Matter standard. Buyers who prefer local recording without ongoing costs should also pass; Ring locks cloud video history, video saving, and parcel detection behind a paid subscription, providing only real-time notifications and live viewing without a plan.

## Australian notes
The Battery Doorbell Plus operates exclusively on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n), meaning you will need adequate 2.4 GHz coverage at your entryway, as double-brick walls or aluminium security doors common in Australian homes can attenuate wireless signals. Ring rates the device for outdoor temperatures from -20°C to 50°C, which covers typical Australian summer conditions.

If you choose to wire the doorbell into existing doorbell wiring for trickle charging rather than recharging the battery manually, it requires an 8 to 24 VAC transformer; check your state's electrical safety regulator if altering domestic wiring. Renters should confirm whether adhesive or screw mounting is permitted under their residential tenancy agreement. When positioning the camera toward shared common property or footpaths, check your state or territory surveillance devices legislation regarding filming neighbours or private spaces. In Australia, Ring backs the unit with a 12-month manufacturer warranty alongside statutory consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law.
