The Arlo Pro 5 2K (model VMC4060P) is an outdoor wire-free security camera designed for residential surveillance. According to Arlo, the camera records video at up to 2K HDR resolution across a 160-degree viewing angle, accompanied by 12x digital zoom and colour night vision supported by an integrated spotlight. Unlike earlier generations that strictly required a proprietary hub or operated solely on single-band connections, Arlo states the Pro 5 connects directly to home routers with dual-band Wi-Fi supporting both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz frequencies. The unit also incorporates two-way audio, motion detection, and an internal siren.

## Who it suits

The Arlo Pro 5 2K suits homeowners and renters seeking an outdoor camera that avoids permanent power cabling. Its rechargeable battery allows placement on exterior walls, fences, or carports without running cables. It also suits households using ecosystems like Amazon Alexa, Google Home, or Samsung SmartThings, as Arlo confirms direct integration with these platforms.

However, users who want a subscription-free security system should consider alternative options. According to Arlo, cloud video recording, 30-day event history, custom activity zones, and intelligent notifications (identifying people, animals, vehicles, and packages) require an ongoing Arlo Secure subscription. While pairing the camera with a separate Arlo SmartHub allows local USB or microSD storage, viewing recordings without a subscription is limited. In addition, Apple HomeKit integration requires an Arlo SmartHub rather than connecting directly over home Wi-Fi.

## Australian notes

The camera is sold by Australian retailers with local compliance and a 12-month manufacturer express warranty. Because the camera runs entirely on rechargeable battery power and recharges with a magnetic cable, installation does not require mains 240V wiring or a licensed electrician.

For renters, the wire-free setup minimises property alterations, though fixing the mounting bracket to exterior walls may require landlord approval; verify your state or territory residential tenancy laws before drilling. Homeowners positioning cameras facing boundary lines, common property, or public footpaths should also consult their state's surveillance devices legislation and privacy guidelines to ensure compliant camera placement. Finally, while the camera supports 5 GHz networks for higher throughput close to the access point, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi remains more effective for penetrating standard Australian double-brick, brick veneer, and insulated external walls.
