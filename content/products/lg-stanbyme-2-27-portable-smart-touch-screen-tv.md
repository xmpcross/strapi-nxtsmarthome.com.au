The LG StanbyME 2 (model 27LX6TDGA) is a 27-inch portable smart display featuring a 1440p QHD touch screen mounted on a movable wheeled stand. LG positions the device as an adaptable household screen that can be detached from its rolling base to operate flat on a tabletop with the included folio cover, or hung using an included wall-mount strap. Powered by LG's alpha 8 AI processor and running the webOS smart platform, the screen provides direct access to video streaming apps alongside wireless mirroring via Apple AirPlay and Google Cast. Because the unit integrates an internal rechargeable battery, LG specifies up to 4 hours of cordless runtime, allowing you to move content around the house away from power outlets.

## Who it suits
This unit suits households and apartment occupants wanting a movable screen to roll between the kitchen bench, home gym, and bedside. The touch panel and included folio cover cater well to users who interact directly with apps, view digital recipes, or sketch on screen. It also fits viewers whose media intake consists primarily of on-demand platforms such as Netflix, Disney+, and Prime Video, or who frequently mirror video from a smartphone or laptop.

Conversely, traditional broadcast television viewers should look elsewhere. The display lacks a built-in terrestrial TV tuner, meaning free-to-air broadcasts cannot be tuned directly via a coaxial antenna lead. Serious gamers and home theatre enthusiasts may find the 60Hz refresh rate and standard LED contrast limiting compared to conventional living-room screens. Those wanting wired personal audio should also take note, as the lack of a 3.5mm headphone jack requires Bluetooth headphones instead.

## Australian notes
The Australian model (27LX6TDGA) is supplied with a local AC power adaptor to recharge the internal battery. Because the display omits an aerial tuner, watching Australian free-to-air stations relies entirely on official network catch-up apps or mobile casting over Wi-Fi 5, making dependable whole-home wireless coverage essential. If you intend to use the included strap and wall bracket rather than the wheeled floor stand in a rental property, check your state tenancy legislation and lease conditions regarding wall fixings before drilling. Australian retailers supply the StanbyME 2 with LG's standard one-year manufacturer warranty, which applies alongside your statutory consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law.
