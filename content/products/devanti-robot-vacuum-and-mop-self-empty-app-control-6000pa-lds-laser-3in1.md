The Devanti Robot Vacuum and Mop (model RVAC-Q20-STA-6K-BK) is a three-in-one floor-cleaning device designed to sweep, vacuum, and mop residential floors. According to manufacturer specifications from Devanti Australia, the unit provides up to 6,000 Pa of suction power and navigates living areas using LDS laser guidance alongside SLAM mapping. Measuring 30.6 cm across and 9.8 cm tall, the robot features an onboard 300 ml dustbox and a 200 ml water tank, backed by a HEPA filtration system. Once cleaning concludes, it returns to an included docking station that automatically evacuates debris into a 1.5-litre collection chamber. Operation is handled either through a mobile smartphone application or an included handheld physical remote control.

## Who it suits

According to product documentation, this robot suits households with hard floors, laminate, tiles, or low-pile carpets seeking routine automated cleaning. The inclusion of a physical remote control makes it an option for occupants who prefer not to manage floor cleaning entirely through a smartphone. With an advertised battery run time of up to 155 minutes, it offers sufficient operating endurance for standard residential floor plans.

However, households with extensive carpeted areas should take note of its mop implementation. The mopping system does not feature automatic pad lifting when crossing carpet. Devanti notes that users must manually configure no-mop zones inside the companion application to prevent water contact on rugs. In addition, manufacturer documentation explicitly states that this series is not suitable for deep shag rugs. Buyers seeking voice automation will also need to look elsewhere, as the official specifications list no integrations for Matter, Google Home, Apple Home, Amazon Alexa, or Home Assistant.

## Australian notes

Devanti distributes this model across several Australian retail marketplaces, including Bunnings, JB Hi-Fi, and Kogan, backed by a standard one-year warranty. The package includes an external power adapter for the auto-emptying base station, though specific Australian plug standard markings and voltage tolerances are not published on the official specification sheet. Always check your state or territory electrical safety regulator for compliance requirements regarding imported power equipment. Furthermore, while mobile app control is supported, Devanti does not document whether the internal radio supports 5 GHz or is restricted to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi bands.
