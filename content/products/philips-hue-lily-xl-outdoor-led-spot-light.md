The Philips Hue Lily XL is an outdoor spotlight designed to expand an existing Hue low-voltage garden lighting setup. According to Philips Hue Australia, the fixture features a 15W integrated LED producing up to 1,060 lumens at 4000K (or 950 lumens at 2700K) across a 60° beam angle. It supports White and Color Ambiance, providing tunable white light from 2000K to 6500K alongside 16 million colours. Built for garden beds, decks, or wall mounting, the unit is packaged with a ground spike, a mounting bracket, a 2.5-metre extension cable, and a low-voltage T-connector.

## Who it suits

This spotlight suits Australian households that already operate a Philips Hue outdoor system or have a compatible 24V Hue power supply unit (available in 40W or 100W configurations). Because it is sold as an extension unit, it does not include a power supply or a Philips Hue Bridge. According to the manufacturer, a Hue Bridge is required to configure automations, timers, remote access, and platform integrations. It is suitable for lighting larger garden features, paths, or walls where the output of smaller garden spots may be insufficient. Buyers seeking a standalone 240V mains fitting, or those who prefer to avoid proprietary hubs, should look elsewhere.

## Australian notes

In Australia, the Lily XL operates on Philips Hue's Class III Safe Extra Low Voltage (24V DC) system. The separate power supply connects to a standard 220–240V Australian outdoor mains outlet. Because the spotlight runs on 24V low-voltage cabling rather than direct 240V mains wiring, Philips Hue states it does not require hardwired electrical work to install; however, check your state's electrical safety regulator regarding outdoor socket installations and cabling requirements.

The fixture holds an IP65 rating for protection against water jets and dust, and the manufacturer lists an operating temperature range between -20 °C and 40 °C. The unit communicates via Zigbee and Bluetooth rather than direct Wi-Fi. Connected through the Hue Bridge, it supports Matter, Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Samsung SmartThings without requiring a subscription. Philips Hue Australia specifies a two-year manufacturer warranty.
