The Nanoleaf Essentials Matter Smart Bulb E27 is a mains-powered LED globe designed for connected lighting setups. Operating on standard Australian 220–240V fixtures with an Edison screw (E27) base, the bulb consumes 8.5W while outputting between 806 and 900 lumens on average, reaching up to 1,100 lumens at peak output, according to Nanoleaf. It produces over 16 million colours alongside tunable white temperatures ranging from 2700K to 6500K with a colour rendering index (CRI) of 90 or greater. Because the bulb is built around the Matter standard, it integrates directly with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, and Home Assistant without locking users into a single brand app or requiring a paid subscription.

## Who it suits

This bulb suits homeowners and renters who want versatile coloured and tunable white lighting without rewiring their property. Because it uses a standard screw-in E27 base, renters can install it into floor lamps or ceiling sockets and take it with them when moving. It is well-suited to households building out multi-platform smart home setups via Matter, allowing different family members to control lights using their preferred assistant, whether that is Siri, Google Assistant, or Alexa. However, buyers should skip this globe if they want outdoor lighting, as product documentation specifies it is strictly rated for indoor use. It is also unsuitable for rooms wired with traditional in-wall dimmer switches; Nanoleaf notes that dimming must be handled exclusively via voice or app controls between 1% and 100%.

## Australian notes

In Australia, the bulb is sold through local retailers including JB Hi-Fi, Bunnings, and Officeworks, and comes with a two-year manufacturer warranty. The globe operates on 220–240V mains power and draws 8.5W. For wireless connectivity, Wi-Fi models require a standard 2.4 GHz network band; dual-band routers must have 2.4 GHz active, as 5 GHz connections are not supported. If purchasing the Thread-enabled hardware variant, Nanoleaf notes that an active Thread border router is required on the local network for pairing and control. If you intend to install this globe into fixtures where existing lighting controls include wall-mounted dimmers or specialised switching, consult a licensed electrician or check your state's electrical safety regulator to ensure compatibility.
