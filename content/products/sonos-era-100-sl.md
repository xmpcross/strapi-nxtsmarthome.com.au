The Sonos Era 100 SL is a mains-powered wireless speaker designed for standalone audio playback, stereo pairing, or use as rear surround channels in a Sonos home theatre configuration. Per Sonos and Australian retailer listings, the unit incorporates three Class-D digital amplifiers powering two angled tweeters for stereo separation alongside a single mid-woofer. Unlike the standard Era 100, the 'SL' variant completely omits internal microphones. Wireless connectivity includes dual-band Wi-Fi 6 across both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands, Bluetooth 5.3, and Apple AirPlay 2. Sonos notes that external audio inputs via the rear USB-C port and wired Ethernet networking both require proprietary adapters sold separately.

## Who it suits
This speaker suits listeners who want multi-room music streaming but prefer hardware-level privacy without listening microphones in private areas such as bedrooms or home offices. It also suits households with a compatible Sonos soundbar seeking dedicated rear channels, as Sonos confirms two units can pair together for home theatre surround sound. Buyers looking for hands-free voice assistance on the unit itself should skip this model, as it cannot receive direct voice commands without external smart devices. Additionally, households wanting to plug in an analogue source such as a turntable must account for the separate adapter requirement, and note that both line-in and Bluetooth playback are disabled whenever the speaker is configured as a surround rear.

## Australian notes
Australian retailer listings from JB Hi-Fi and The Good Guys confirm the speaker ships with a standard Australian 2-metre power cable connected to an internal 100–240 V, 50/60 Hz universal power supply. For home networking across Australian residences, the dual-band Wi-Fi 6 radio connects to either 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz bands on local routers. Sonos provides a 12-month manufacturer warranty through authorised Australian retail channels. Smart platform support covers Apple Home via AirPlay 2, while external Amazon Alexa devices can target playback using the Sonos skill; however, Matter and Google Cast are not supported. Australian users should also note that Trueplay room acoustic calibration requires an Apple iOS device, as this microphone-free speaker does not support Android-based tuning.
