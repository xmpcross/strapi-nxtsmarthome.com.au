The Homey Bridge is a compact smart-home hub that connects physical wireless accessories to the cloud-based Homey automation platform. According to manufacturer specifications, the disc-shaped transceiver includes radios for Zigbee, Z-Wave Plus, Bluetooth Low Energy, and 433 MHz, along with an integrated infrared transmitter. Unlike dedicated local controllers, the bridge does not process smart-home automations on the device itself. Instead, it acts as an antenna that relays signals between your household accessories and Homey Cloud across your home Wi-Fi network.

## Who it suits
This hub suits homeowners and renters who want to unify older and newer wireless standards within a single smartphone application without configuring dedicated local server hardware. If your home has a mix of infrared air-conditioner remotes, 433 MHz window blinds, Z-Wave door sensors, and Zigbee light globes, the Homey Bridge allows you to control them from one place. It also suits existing Homey Pro owners who need an additional multi-protocol satellite to extend wireless radio coverage into a distant room or garage.

Conversely, households seeking a completely local smart home should skip this model. Because all flow automations and device communications are processed through Homey Cloud, the system stops running automated routines if your internet connection drops out. Furthermore, Homey limits free cloud accounts to a maximum of five connected devices. If your setup exceeds five devices or requires advanced logic, variables, and Homey Insights, you must pay for an ongoing Homey Premium subscription. Power users wanting Thread, Matter controller capability, community-developed local plugins, or native Apple HomeKit integration will also find the bridge unsuitable, as those features are restricted to the Homey Pro ecosystem.

## Australian notes
Retailer listings confirm the Australian variant carries the model number HOMEY-BRIDGE-AU-01 and includes a compliant Australian USB power plug adapter. The unit is powered by an included 5V/1A USB power supply connected to a micro-USB port on the base. For wireless connectivity, the bridge operates exclusively on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n) and does not support 5 GHz networks or wired Ethernet connections, so ensure your home router has a dedicated or split 2.4 GHz band active during setup.

For platform compatibility, the bridge supports Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa through official cloud integrations. However, native Apple Home, Matter, and SmartThings support are absent on the bridge hardware. The Z-Wave Plus antenna supports Australian regional frequencies according to local retailer specifications. When purchased through authorised Australian retailers, the unit includes a two-year manufacturer warranty, backed by statutory consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law.
