The Google Nest Thermostat (regulatory model G4CVZ) is a wall-mounted climate controller designed for 24V low-voltage heating and cooling systems. According to Google's technical specifications, the unit features an 84 mm diameter circular body with a 2.4-inch QVGA IPS colour LCD behind a mirrored glass cover. Connectivity includes dual-band 802.11a/b/g/n Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Low Energy. The device incorporates a Soli radar sensor for motion detection alongside ambient light, temperature, and humidity sensors. Google notes that it operates without a paid subscription, running remote temperature adjustments, usage insights, and Auto-Away routines directly through the Google Home platform.

## Who it suits

This thermostat suits homeowners with ducted, low-voltage 24V AC heating and cooling infrastructure who want broad platform interoperability. Following official firmware updates, the device supports Matter, enabling it to work across Apple Home, Home Assistant, SmartThings, and Amazon Alexa in addition to native Google Home setups. It also suits users wanting an automated thermostat that does not lock core scheduling or remote access behind a recurring subscription fee.

However, it is not suitable for homes relying on standard split-system air conditioners. According to technical documentation, the unit does not include an infrared blaster and cannot directly emulate handheld air conditioner remotes. It is also unsuitable for users wanting to programme schedules directly at the wall, as temperature routines must be managed through the Google Home mobile app.

## Australian notes

Google does not officially sell or distribute the Nest Thermostat in Australia, and the Australian Google Store does not list the device. Mainstream Australian electronics retailers do not stock it, leaving local availability limited to third-party grey-market importers on marketplaces such as Kogan.com and eBay Australia. Consequently, there is no official manufacturer warranty in Australia, and warranty recourse is restricted to the individual importer's policies.

From a hardware perspective, the unit is designed for North American 20–30V AC low-voltage HVAC systems using up to six wire terminals (R, C, W, Y, G, O/B). It is not designed to control standard Australian 240V mains-switched ducted setups or split systems without custom 24V transformers, relays, or the Nest Power Connector. Before undertaking any fixed electrical modifications to climate systems, check your state's electrical safety regulator and tenancy rules to confirm licensing and compliance requirements.
