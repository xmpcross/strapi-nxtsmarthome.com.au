The Mirabella Genio 9W Wi-Fi Dimmable Downlight is an LED ceiling luminaire intended for residential smart lighting setups. According to Mirabella International, the fitting delivers up to 850 lumens of brightness (listed as 750 lumens by Bunnings) and offers both full RGB colour adjustment across 16 million colours and tunable white output from 2700K to 6500K. The fixture includes a 1.2-metre lead with a standard Australian plug and is designed to fit ceiling cut-outs between 90 mm and 95 mm.

## Who it suits

According to retailer and manufacturer data, this downlight suits homeowners and renters whose ceilings already feature recessed power sockets. Because each unit comes with a pre-wired flex and plug, it can be plugged directly into an existing socket base in the roof cavity without altering household wiring. It is well suited to households that manage their automation using Google Assistant or Amazon Alexa and want colour and scheduling controls through the Mirabella Genio app without paying subscription fees.

It is not suitable for users invested in the Apple Home or Matter ecosystems, as neither platform is supported. It should also be skipped if you plan to keep existing rotary or mechanical wall dimmers; Mirabella notes that brightness must be adjusted strictly via the companion app or compatible voice assistants.

## Australian notes

Mirabella fits the downlight with a 1.2-metre lead and standard Australian plug. While plugging the unit into an existing surface socket is simple, installing new ceiling socket bases or altering mains wiring is generally work for a licensed electrician; check your state's electrical safety regulator.

The light carries an IC-4 rating, meaning it can safely abut or be covered by building insulation according to manufacturer documentation, alongside an IP44 rating for moisture resistance. Networking is restricted to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Mirabella notes that users with dual-band Australian home routers may need to separate their 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks or disable the 5 GHz band during initial device pairing. Retail units sold in Australia carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty.
