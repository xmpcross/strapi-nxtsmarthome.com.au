The Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra is an autonomous robot vacuum and mop paired with the multifunctional RockDock Ultra. According to Roborock's Australian specifications, the unit features 10,000 Pa suction and uses PreciSense LiDAR paired with Reactive AI 2.0 obstacle avoidance, which the manufacturer states can recognise up to 73 obstacle types using an RGB camera. For mopping, it employs the VibraRise 3.0 sonic mopping system operating at 4,000 vibrations per minute. To address corner and perimeter coverage, Roborock adds an extending FlexiArm side brush and a rotating edge mop. The automated base station handles dust emptying, clean water refilling, automated detergent dispensing from a 580 ml reservoir, 60°C hot water mop cleaning, and heated air drying.

## Who it suits

This system suits households with extensive hard flooring, tiles, or floorboards where frequent mopping is beneficial without daily manual pad washing. The automated hot water wash and heated drying cycles minimise manual maintenance. Homes with pets may also benefit from the camera-assisted pet recognition and remote video calling capabilities. 

However, households with dense, high-pile plush carpets should take note: while the mop module lifts automatically by up to 20 mm when carpet is detected, Roborock notes it may still brush against deeper pile fibres. It is also unsuitable for compact spaces or apartments lacking open floor clearance, as the docking station measures 409 × 419 × 470 mm and the total boxed weight exceeds 18 kg.

## Australian notes

Official Australian retailer units are supplied with a standard Australian 220–240V AC power lead. The robot operates exclusively on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks; users with split or single-SSID dual-band routers will need to ensure a 2.4 GHz connection is available during setup, as 5 GHz is unsupported.

Smart-home compatibility is extensive. The S8 MaxV Ultra includes native Matter certification, enabling cross-platform integration across Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Samsung SmartThings. It also features local Roborock integration in Home Assistant and includes an on-device voice assistant ("Hello Rocky"). There are no ongoing subscription fees for core mapping, video, or scheduling features. Local purchases through authorised Australian channels include a standard 12-month Australian manufacturer warranty.
