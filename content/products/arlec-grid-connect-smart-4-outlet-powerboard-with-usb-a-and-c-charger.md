The Arlec Grid Connect Smart 4 Outlet Powerboard with USB A and C Charger (model PB4UHA) is an indoor power accessory designed to automate domestic appliances. According to specifications from Arlec Australia, the unit provides four standard 240V mains sockets that can be powered on or off independently via mobile software, alongside a combined dual-port USB block featuring one USB-A and one USB-C connection. It includes a physical master power switch, status LEDs, and safety overload protection designed to disconnect power if the combined load exceeds the powerboard's maximum rating.

## Who it suits

According to retailer and manufacturer listings, this powerboard suits users who want to schedule or automate several appliances in one place, such as entertainment units, home office desks, or bedside tables. Because each mains socket is addressed separately in the Grid Connect mobile application, you can automate specific devices like lamps or fans while leaving other connected electronics powered. It also suits households using Google Assistant or Amazon Alexa smart speakers for basic voice-triggered power toggling without any ongoing software subscription fees.

It is less suitable for users operating Apple Home, Matter, or Samsung SmartThings platforms, as manufacturer documentation lists no native compatibility with these environments. Those relying on Home Assistant should also note that there is no official integration available. Additionally, households requiring individual scheduling for separate USB devices will find the shared, non-independent USB-A and USB-C switching restrictive.

## Australian notes

The PB4UHA is configured for standard Australian mains power, rated for 230–240V AC at 50Hz with a maximum total load of 10A (2400W) across all connected equipment. Arlec specifies a 0.9-metre power cable terminated with a three-pin Australian side-entry plug, intended to help the connection fit neatly behind furniture.

Wireless pairing operates exclusively over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks; 5 GHz connections are not supported, meaning dual-band routers must have a 2.4 GHz band active. The board is rated solely for indoor use and must not be exposed to outdoor moisture. Because this is a portable plug-in board, installation does not require fixed electrical work; for any broader home wiring questions or safety standards, consult your state's electrical safety regulator. The product is covered by standard statutory consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law.
