The Laser Digital Full HD Media Player (model STB-9000-675) is a compact set-top box designed to receive and decode Australian free-to-air terrestrial television broadcasts. Laser states that the unit decodes high-definition MPEG-4 and MPEG-2 signals, outputting resolutions up to 1080p via HDMI while also providing composite (CVBS) video and stereo RCA audio for older televisions. The device doubles as a personal video recorder (PVR) when paired with an external USB storage drive, allowing users to pause, record, and archive live broadcasts. An included remote control navigates the on-screen display, handles channel editing, and interfaces with the built-in seven-day electronic programme guide (EPG).

## Who it suits
According to Laser, this set-top box is suited to viewers wanting to prolong the life of older analogue televisions or monitors that lack an integrated digital tuner. It also caters to households seeking simple television recording functionality without paying for ongoing subscription services. By plugging a USB flash drive or powered external hard drive into the USB port, users can schedule upcoming programmes directly through the seven-day EPG or start recording immediately with a single button press. Because the STB-9000 features an RF aerial pass-through connection, viewers can route the incoming antenna signal to a secondary digital tuner to watch another station while the box records. Additionally, the unit serves as an offline multimedia player, with Laser confirming playback support for AVI video files, MP3 audio, and JPEG images. Viewers looking for built-in streaming apps like Netflix or ABC iview, twin internal tuners, or Wi-Fi smart controls should look elsewhere, as this device does not support connected streaming.

## Australian notes
Laser specifies that the STB-9000 is built for Australian DVB-T television standards, connecting directly to standard Australian coaxial wall outlets. It operates on Australian 240V mains power with an energy draw under 1W in standby and up to 6W at maximum operation. For PVR recording, Laser notes compatibility with USB storage drives up to 750GB, and recommends that external hard drives use an independent power supply if the USB port cannot supply sufficient current. From a smart-home perspective, the set-top box is completely offline; it lacks network connectivity and does not integrate with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Matter, or Home Assistant. Units purchased through local retailers such as BIG W, JB Hi-Fi, and Bing Lee come backed by Laser's 12-month manufacturer warranty alongside consumer guarantees provided under Australian Consumer Law.
