The Sonos Era 100 is a mains-powered wireless smart speaker designed for standalone or multi-room audio playback. According to Sonos Australia, the unit measures 182.5 mm high by 120 mm wide, weighs 2.02 kg, and houses two angled tweeters, a dedicated mid-woofer, and three Class-D digital amplifiers driven by a quad-core processor. For connectivity, it features dual-band Wi-Fi 6 alongside Bluetooth 5.3, allowing playback from services such as Spotify and Apple Music via the Sonos app or direct device streaming. Physical controls include top-mounted capacitive sliders alongside a dedicated hardware switch to disconnect the built-in microphones.

## Who it suits
This speaker suits listeners who want room-filling audio integrated into the Sonos platform without relying exclusively on Wi-Fi, thanks to the added Bluetooth 5.3 connection. Households that utilise Apple Home via AirPlay 2 or rely on Amazon Alexa for voice commands will find native compatibility built into the unit, according to the manufacturer. It also suits households using both iOS and Android devices, as Sonos states its Trueplay acoustic room-tuning feature works across both operating systems.

Conversely, buyers seeking Google Assistant voice control should look elsewhere, as retailer specifications from The Good Guys confirm Google Assistant is not supported on this model. It is also unsuitable for those needing a portable outdoor speaker, as Sonos confirms it lacks an internal battery and requires continuous mains power. Additionally, existing Sonos One or Play:1 owners cannot pair the Era 100 with those older units for stereo audio, and anyone wanting to plug in an analogue turntable or wired Ethernet cable must purchase separate proprietary USB-C adapters from Sonos.

## Australian notes
Units supplied through authorised Australian retailers, carrying model reference E10G1AU1BLK, include an internal 100–240 V (50/60 Hz) power supply and a standard two-metre Australian AC power cable. Wi-Fi compatibility covers both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands over 802.11ax standards, ensuring compatibility with standard Australian home routers. Local retail purchases from stockists such as JB Hi-Fi and The Good Guys come with a one-year manufacturer warranty from Sonos Australia, which sits alongside statutory consumer protections guaranteed under the Australian Consumer Law.
