The TP-Link Tapo P300 is a Wi-Fi power strip designed for indoor household use. According to TP-Link Australia, the unit features three standard Australian 240V mains outlets alongside three USB charging ports, consisting of one USB-C port and two USB-A ports. Unlike traditional power boards, each of the three AC outlets can be switched independently via the Tapo app or physical on-device buttons, although TP-Link notes the USB ports remain continuously powered. The unit connects directly to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi without requiring a dedicated hardware bridge, allowing remote switching, schedules, and timer routines without ongoing subscription fees.

## Who it suits

According to manufacturer specifications, this power strip suits households seeking separate control over multiple appliances located in the one spot, such as desk setups, entertainment units, or bedside tables. Because TP-Link officially supports Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa, it fits mixed-ecosystem households wanting voice commands through Siri, Google Assistant, or Alexa. Renters can also deploy it easily without modifying permanent electrical fixtures. However, households using Matter, Samsung SmartThings, or Home Assistant should note that TP-Link does not list official support for these platforms on the Australian Tapo P300 specification sheet. It is also unsuitable for users wanting to track electricity consumption, as TP-Link confirms the P300 lacks energy-monitoring hardware, or anyone who needs remote switching on USB ports.

## Australian notes

According to TP-Link Australia, the Tapo P300 features a standard Australian 3-pin plug and operates at 230–240V (50/60 Hz) with Regulatory Compliance Mark (RCM) certification. The strip provides an aggregate capacity of 10A and 2400W across its 1.5-metre cord. TP-Link documentation indicates that connected loads across all three sockets must not exceed this total 10A limit, meaning it cannot support high-draw heating appliances or EV charging gear simultaneously. For home networking, the board connects exclusively over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, using Bluetooth only for onboarding. TP-Link covers the P300 with a two-year manufacturer warranty under its Australian Smart Power policy, alongside statutory protections under the Australian Consumer Law. For guidelines on safe power board usage and preventing electrical overloads, check your state's electrical safety regulator.
