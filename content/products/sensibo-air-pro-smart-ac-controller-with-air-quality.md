The Sensibo Air PRO (model SEN-AIRQ-CRL-01) is a compact plug-in controller designed to add smart functionality to existing split-system air conditioners, portable units, and heat pumps. Rather than replacing existing heating and cooling equipment, it uses dual infrared (IR) emitters to communicate with units that rely on a standard handheld remote control. According to Sensibo, the device pairs this remote control functionality with environmental sensors that track temperature, relative humidity, Total Volatile Organic Compounds (TVOC), and calculated carbon dioxide equivalent (eCO₂). It also includes Sensibo’s PureBoost automation feature, which the manufacturer states can cycle the air conditioning fan to circulate air when poor indoor conditions are detected.

## Who it suits

This controller suits Australian renters and homeowners with existing split-system units who want app-based control and scheduling without altering fixed wiring. Because it communicates via infrared signals and plugs into an ordinary power point, it does not require an electrician or permanent modifications. Check your tenancy agreement regarding wall mounting with adhesive pads. It is well suited to multi-platform households, as manufacturer documentation confirms integration with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, and Home Assistant via open API. 

However, it is not suitable for homes with central ducted systems governed strictly by hardwired wall controllers. Buyers seeking dedicated indoor air monitoring should also note that Sensibo derives eCO₂ from VOC readings rather than using a dedicated non-dispersive infrared (NDIR) sensor, and it does not measure airborne particulate matter such as PM2.5 or PM10.

## Australian notes

Retail listings confirm the Sensibo Air PRO ships with an Australian-compatible 5V 1A USB mains power adapter and a 1.0 m to 1.2 m micro-USB lead. Because there is no internal rechargeable battery, the unit must remain plugged into a standard 230V mains socket within line of sight of your air conditioner's receiver. Network pairing requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi connection; specifications confirm that 5 GHz bands are unsupported, though Bluetooth Low Energy is used for the initial setup.

Purchases through authorised Australian retailers come with a one-year manufacturer warranty, alongside consumer guarantees provided under Australian Consumer Law. Core features function out of the box, though long-term trend data beyond real-time readings and automated anti-mould drying cycles require the optional Sensibo Plus subscription.
