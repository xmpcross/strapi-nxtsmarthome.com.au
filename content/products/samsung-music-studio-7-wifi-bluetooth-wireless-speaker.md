The Samsung Music Studio 7 (model HW-LS70H/XY in black and HW-LS71H/XY in white) is a mains-powered bookshelf smart speaker designed for standalone music listening or television audio integration. According to Samsung Australia, the unit features a 3.1.1 channel layout comprising five drivers: left, centre, and right channels, an up-firing height driver, an integrated woofer, and a super tweeter. Samsung states the speaker provides up to 150W of total amplifier power, with audio support covering high-resolution audio up to 24-bit/96kHz, SpaceFit Sound Pro room calibration, and both wired and wireless Dolby Atmos decoding.

## Who it suits

This speaker suits households looking for an alternative to a traditional multi-box soundbar or audio receiver setup. Because it includes an HDMI eARC port and an optical audio input, it connects directly to television setups to handle both streaming media and TV audio. Retailer and manufacturer specifications confirm the speaker includes Amazon Alexa built-in, Works with Google Home via Google Cast, and works with Apple AirPlay 2 and Samsung SmartThings. This wide streaming protocol support fits mixed-platform households that want voice control and multi-room audio capabilities without locking into a single brand ecosystem. It also suits owners of compatible 2022 to 2026 Samsung TVs who want to utilise Samsung's Q-Symphony feature, which plays audio synchronised across both the television and the speaker.

Conversely, those wanting portable sound should skip this model, as it requires continuous mains AC power and does not contain a battery. Users seeking pass-through HDMI switching for games consoles or set-top boxes should also look elsewhere, as the single HDMI connection functions solely for eARC audio input from the screen. Additionally, the onboard USB port cannot play media files from flash drives, as Samsung designates it for service only.

## Australian notes

In Australia, the Music Studio 7 is supplied with a standard detachable Australian mains AC power cable and carries a 12-month manufacturer warranty from Samsung Australia, alongside statutory protections under the Australian Consumer Law. The unit features dual-band 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi; however, Samsung explicitly notes that multi-speaker Group Play synchronisation requires connected units to operate on the 5 GHz band. No recurring subscription is required to use the speaker hardware or the SmartThings app, though external music streaming subscriptions remain separate. Matter and native Apple HomeKit smart home control are not listed in the manufacturer specifications.
