The TP-Link Tapo C610 KIT is an outdoor wire-free security camera package featuring a motorised pan-and-tilt camera unit and an external Tapo A201 solar panel. According to TP-Link Australia, the camera records in 2K 3MP resolution (2304 × 1296) and uses a 6,500 mAh internal rechargeable battery charged via an external USB-C solar panel. The camera provides 340° horizontal pan and 90° vertical tilt, allowing 360° visual coverage across an outdoor space. Night footage is handled either via traditional black-and-white infrared LEDs up to 10 metres or in full colour using dual integrated white spotlights. An IP65 weatherproof casing protects the unit against rain and dust.

## Who it suits

This kit suits users who need broad outdoor area coverage without running mains electrical cabling to eaves or garden posts. Because the solar panel includes a 4-metre extension cable, the panel can be positioned in clear sunlight while keeping the camera shaded under a verandah. Renters and homeowners who prefer avoiding monthly cloud fees will appreciate that local storage is supported via a microSD card slot accommodating cards up to 512 GB (card sold separately). Features such as on-device AI person detection, automatic tracking, two-way audio, and custom sirens function out of the box without a Tapo Care plan.

However, it will not suit users seeking continuous 24/7 surveillance or integration into a local Network Attached Storage (NAS) system. To preserve battery power, TP-Link confirms the unit sleeps between events, which disables continuous recording and ONVIF or RTSP streams. It also lacks compatibility with Apple Home, Matter, and Home Assistant.

## Australian notes

The Tapo C610 KIT connects strictly to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks (802.11b/g/n), meaning dual-band home routers common across Australia must have a 2.4 GHz band active. Voice control and live feeds are supported across Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa smart displays.

TP-Link covers the product with a 1-year limited warranty in Australia, which sits alongside statutory guarantees provided by the Australian Consumer Law (ACL). When installing the camera and aiming its pan-and-tilt lens, be aware of surveillance and privacy regulations regarding neighbouring properties and public footpaths; check your state or territory privacy laws before mounting. If attaching the solar panel or camera bracket to rented property, check your state tenancy legislation regarding wall fixings.
