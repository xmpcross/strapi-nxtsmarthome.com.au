The Tuya Wi-Fi Temperature and Humidity Sensor is an indoor hygrometer and thermometer designed for wireless environmental monitoring. Based on specifications published on Tuya Expo and Australian retailer listings such as Kogan and Big W Marketplace, the device features a built-in LCD display showing real-time temperature, humidity levels, the current time, battery status, and Wi-Fi signal strength. The sensor measures temperatures from -10°C to 55°C (with an accuracy of ±0.3°C to ±1°C) and relative humidity between 0% and 99% RH (accuracy ±3% to ±5% RH). Because it connects directly to local Wi-Fi, it allows remote access and automations via the Tuya Smart or Smart Life mobile applications without requiring an auxiliary hub.

## Who it suits
This sensor suits Australian renters and homeowners seeking an entry-level indoor monitor that functions without extra bridge hardware. It is suitable for monitoring standard rooms, nurseries, wine cabinets, or wardrobes, provided users place it near eye level; community feedback notes that the LCD display can suffer from poor contrast when viewed from an angle.

It works well for households already using the Tuya Smart, Smart Life, Amazon Alexa, or Google Assistant ecosystems. Home Assistant users can also integrate it using Tuya cloud or local integrations. However, it will not suit households built around Apple Home (HomeKit), Samsung SmartThings, or Matter, as these platforms are not natively supported. Anyone requiring rapid, continuous updates for climate automations should look elsewhere. User manuals and community reports indicate the device enters a deep-sleep cycle between transmissions to conserve battery life, reporting data only periodically (typically every 30 to 120 minutes) or upon significant threshold changes.

## Australian notes
Because the sensor is battery-powered—running on standard AAA batteries rather than mains electricity—it does not require an Australian electrical plug, fixed wiring, or an electrician to install. Some hardware revisions also feature a secondary 5V Micro-USB or USB-C auxiliary power port. Note that the unit is strictly designed for indoor use and carries no outdoor weather resistance.

Wi-Fi connectivity is limited strictly to 2.4 GHz (802.11 b/g/n) networks; dual-band routers common in Australian homes must broadcast an active 2.4 GHz band for setup. Tuya functions as a platform and chipset provider rather than a direct consumer brand, so there is no direct Australian manufacturer warranty. Buyers must rely on merchant warranties offered by individual third-party marketplace sellers on platforms like Kogan, Big W, and Amazon AU under Australian Consumer Law.
