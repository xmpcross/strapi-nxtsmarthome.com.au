The Hisense HS2100 is an entry-level 2.1-channel audio system comprising a soundbar and a wireless 5.25-inch subwoofer. According to Hisense Australia, the setup delivers up to 240W of total maximum audio output across two full-range soundbar drivers and the separate subwoofer. It handles Dolby Audio formats, including Dolby Digital and Dolby Digital Plus, alongside DTS Virtual:X processing for simulated surround sound. Physical inputs include an HDMI ARC output, an optical digital audio port, a 3.5mm AUX jack, and a USB port that supports MP3, WMA, FLAC, and WAV playback. Local wireless streaming from mobile devices is supported via Bluetooth 5.3.

## Who it suits
This setup suits homeowners and renters who want to improve standard television audio without running audio cables across their living area. The wireless connection between the soundbar and the subwoofer eliminates the need for interconnecting leads, though both units require separate mains power. It also suits owners of compatible Hisense televisions who want centralised remote controls via the brand's EzPlay feature.

Shoppers seeking a connected smart home setup should look elsewhere. The HS2100 contains no Wi-Fi hardware, meaning it does not connect to Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, SmartThings, Matter, or Home Assistant. You cannot stream music directly via Apple AirPlay, Google Cast, or Spotify Connect over a local home network. In addition, home theatre enthusiasts wanting immersive height effects will need to consider higher-tier models, as the HS2100 does not decode Dolby Atmos or feature dedicated upward-firing drivers.

## Australian notes
According to the manufacturer manual, the soundbar and subwoofer run on 100–240V AC mains power at 50/60Hz and include detachable Australian AC power cords in the box. Each component draws approximately 20W during typical operation and under 0.5W on standby. Because the wireless subwoofer requires its own mains feed, make sure you have an accessible wall socket near your intended placement spot.

Renters considering wall-mounting the 1.5kg soundbar using the bundled bracket should check their residential tenancy agreement or state tenancy authority rules regarding drilling into walls. Australian retailer listings indicate the HS2100 comes with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Note that Hisense Australia lists this specific model as discontinued on its local website, which may affect long-term availability.
