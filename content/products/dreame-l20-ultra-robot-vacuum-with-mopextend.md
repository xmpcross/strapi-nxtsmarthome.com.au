The Dreame L20 Ultra (model DRE-L20U) is an automated robotic vacuum and mopping cleaner. Dreame states that the robot provides up to 7,000 Pa of suction alongside dual rotating DuoScrub mopping pads. Its main mechanical feature is MopExtend technology, where the right-hand mop pad physically extends outward to clean flush along skirting boards and into corners. For households with carpeting, Dreame notes the mop pads can lift by up to 10.5 mm or automatically detach inside the base station to keep carpets dry. The base station manages dust emptying into a 3.2-litre bag, clean and dirty water tank exchanges, automated detergent refilling, and hot-air pad drying. Obstacle management relies on Pathfinder LDS lidar paired with an AI RGB camera and 3D structured light sensors.

## Who it suits

This unit suits households with extensive hard flooring that want thorough mopping right to the edge of walls. Homes with a mix of thick carpets and hard floors benefit from the dock's ability to mechanically leave the mop pads behind before vacuuming carpeted zones. Pet owners will find the 7,000 Pa suction and self-emptying base practical for routine hair collection without manual bin cleaning. However, buyers with restricted floor space should measure carefully, as the docking station is bulky and requires vertical clearance to remove the water tanks. Those seeking heated-water mop washing or complex home automation routines will need to look elsewhere, as the dock washes in cold water and assistant support is basic.

## Australian notes

Official Australian stock operates on standard domestic power at 220–240 V (50/60 Hz) and connects via an Australian Type I three-pin power cord. Dreame Australia provides a three-year manufacturer warranty when bought through authorised domestic retailers. Network setup requires a dedicated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi connection, as the hardware is incompatible with 5 GHz Wi-Fi bands. The mobile app does not require a paid ongoing subscription to access mapping, scheduling, or live camera functions. For smart home ecosystems, voice integration is limited to native Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant commands for basic tasks like starting or docking, with basic Siri Shortcuts available on iOS. It lacks native compatibility with Apple Home, Matter, and Samsung SmartThings.
