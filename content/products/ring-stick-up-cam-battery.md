The Ring Stick Up Cam Battery is a wire-free indoor and outdoor security camera designed for flexible placement on flat surfaces or walls. According to Ring, the camera records 1080p HD video, includes night vision, and provides a 130-degree diagonal field of view alongside two-way audio with noise cancellation. Power is supplied by a removable, rechargeable lithium-ion battery pack, removing the requirement to run fixed power cabling. The manufacturer also offers compatibility with an optional solar panel or mains power adaptor for continuous operation.

## Who it suits
This camera suits renters and homeowners looking for a straightforward, wire-free security camera that mounts without permanent electrical modifications. Because the unit rests on an integrated stand or fastens using standard mounting screws, it is well suited to rental properties where drilling into brick or altering mains wiring is prohibited. It also fits households already invested in the Amazon Alexa smart-home ecosystem, as it allows live-view streaming directly to compatible Echo Show displays.

Conversely, households seeking subscription-free local video storage should look elsewhere. Ring cameras do not include a microSD card slot or onboard base station storage; saving and reviewing recorded footage requires an ongoing Ring subscription plan. Users wanting integration with Apple Home or Google Home setups will also find compatibility limited, as Ring prioritises Amazon Alexa.

## Australian notes
The Stick Up Cam Battery connects exclusively over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks (802.11 b/g/n) and requires an upload speed of at least 2 Mbps for optimal operation, according to Ring. If mounted outdoors on boundary fences or brick exteriors, ensure adequate Wi-Fi coverage reaches the mounting position.

The battery recharges via a standard USB cable, meaning no licensed electrical work is necessary for standard setup. However, renters should confirm mounting permissions with their landlord or rental provider before fastening brackets to external surfaces. When positioning the camera outdoors, think about your neighbours' privacy and check your state's surveillance device laws on recording common areas or adjacent properties.

Ring rates the unit's operating temperatures between -20°C and 50°C, which covers most Australian climates, though Ring advises protecting the housing from prolonged direct sunlight. In Australia, Ring provides a one-year limited manufacturer warranty alongside consumer guarantees provided under the Australian Consumer Law.
