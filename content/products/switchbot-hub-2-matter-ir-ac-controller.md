The SwitchBot Hub 2 is a compact 4-in-1 desktop device functioning as a Matter bridge, universal infrared (IR) controller, climate monitor, and dual-button scene trigger. According to SwitchBot, the unit connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi to expose traditional IR-controlled appliances—such as split-system air conditioners, fans, and televisions—alongside Bluetooth SwitchBot accessories to major smart home ecosystems. It features an auto-dimming LED front display indicating ambient temperature and humidity, measured by an external sensor module located along the power cable. Two capacitive touch buttons on the front face allow users to trigger preset automations or scenes manually.

## Who it suits
According to retailer specifications, this hub suits Australian renters and homeowners seeking smart control over non-smart split-system air conditioners or home entertainment setups without modifying electrical fixtures. Renters can automate cooling without landlord approval or permanent alterations, as the Hub 2 communicates with appliances via line-of-sight infrared signals. It also suits households running Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, or Home Assistant that want to expose compatible SwitchBot sub-devices to their primary ecosystem using Matter over Wi-Fi. SwitchBot confirms that remote access, scheduling, and standard automations require no paid subscription. However, households requiring Thread border routing, Zigbee, or Z-Wave networking should look elsewhere, as the Hub 2 lacks those radios.

## Australian notes
Authorised Australian stock from retailers such as JB Hi-Fi, Harvey Norman, and distributor Clever Gear includes an Australian-compatible 5V/2A power adapter alongside the required two-metre USB-C cable. SwitchBot notes that the temperature and humidity sensors are embedded directly into this supplied cable; substituting an ordinary aftermarket USB-C lead powers the device but disables all climate tracking. The unit contains no internal backup battery and powers down immediately if unplugged from mains electricity. Units purchased through local Australian retailers come with a 12-month manufacturer warranty, which sits alongside consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law. Wi-Fi setup requires a 2.4 GHz wireless network (802.11 b/g/n), as 5 GHz bands and wired Ethernet connections are unsupported.
