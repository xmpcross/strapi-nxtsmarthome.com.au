The TP-Link Tapo C500 is a mains-powered outdoor security camera designed for continuous perimeter monitoring. According to TP-Link, the unit features a 1080p Full HD sensor paired with motorised pan-and-tilt hardware that delivers 360-degree horizontal and 130-degree vertical visual coverage. The manufacturer states that on-device smart artificial intelligence detects persons and automatically tracks movement across its field of view. The camera includes two-way audio, a customisable sound alarm, infrared night vision up to 30 metres, and an IP65 weatherproof housing. Footage can be saved locally to a microSD card up to 512 GB or uploaded to the optional Tapo Care cloud service.

## Who it suits
This camera suits homeowners and tenants looking for automated perimeter coverage without ongoing subscription fees. Because the C500 supports standalone local storage on a microSD card, you can view live feeds and record continuous 24/7 footage entirely within the Tapo app without paying for cloud plans. It is also suitable for households using Amazon Alexa or Google Assistant smart displays to view camera feeds on demand.

Conversely, users needing high-resolution identification of distant details or vehicle licence plates should consider higher-resolution options, as the camera is limited to 1080p at 15 frames per second. It is also unsuitable for remote boundary fences or sheds without mains power, as it does not run on batteries.

## Australian notes
The Tapo C500 requires continuous power from the included power adapter plugged into an Australian mains outlet. If your installation requires fitting a new outdoor weatherproof socket or routing cables through external walls, consult a licensed electrician and check your state's electrical safety regulator. Renters should check their tenancy agreement and obtain landlord approval before drilling mounting holes into exterior brickwork or eaves.

Because the camera pans 360 degrees, consider camera placement carefully. Pointing surveillance equipment into neighbouring backyards or windows can trigger state-specific surveillance device legislation; verify local privacy and surveillance laws with your state or territory justice department.

Network connectivity relies entirely on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, as the camera lacks an RJ45 Ethernet port. Solid brick or corrugated iron construction common in Australian homes can reduce Wi-Fi signal outdoors, so test reception at your chosen mounting location before drilling. When purchased through authorised Australian retailers, TP-Link hardware includes a two-year manufacturer warranty alongside standard statutory guarantees under Australian Consumer Law.
