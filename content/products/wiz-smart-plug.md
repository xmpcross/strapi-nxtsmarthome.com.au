The WiZ Smart Plug (by Signify) is a Wi-Fi-connected plug adapter designed to automate standard household appliances and lamps. According to WiZ, the plug connects directly to a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network without requiring a dedicated hub or bridge. Once inserted into a standard power outlet, users can turn connected electrical devices on and off using the WiZ smartphone app, automated schedules, or voice control via Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Siri Shortcuts. The plug does not require any fixed electrical modification, making it a simple retrofit option for existing fittings.

## Who it suits
According to the manufacturer's documentation, this plug is designed for users who want to control simple mains-powered equipment such as floor lamps, pedestal fans, and decorative lighting. Because it operates through the standard WiZ ecosystem alongside WiZ smart bulbs, it suits households already using WiZ lighting who want to integrate basic appliances into the same room groups and routines.

Renters will find this plug particularly suitable because it adds automation to lamps and electronics without requiring tenancy approval or any wiring changes. It also suits anyone looking to establish basic daily timer routines—such as switching on morning appliances or cycling lights while away—without needing a proprietary bridge.

However, households wanting detailed power consumption analysis should note that this standard Australian model does not report live energy usage metrics in the WiZ app. Furthermore, like all switched smart adapters, it only suits appliances equipped with mechanical on/off switches that retain their active state when mains power is cycled, rather than modern digital soft-touch buttons.

## Australian notes
The Australian version of the WiZ Smart Plug features a standard three-pin Type I plug complying with AS/NZS 3112 standards. WiZ rates the unit for a 240V supply with a maximum current capacity of 10A (equivalent to 2,400W), accommodating most standard domestic appliances.

WiZ specifies that the device connects via 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, which is standard across Australian domestic NBN modems and routers. According to Signify's technical documentation, the platform supports integration with Google Assistant, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, and Matter-compatible ecosystems, as well as iOS voice control via Siri Shortcuts. Because it is an uninstalled plug-in adapter, it does not require licensed electrical work. For general electrical safety standards, check your state's electrical safety regulator. Local warranty support is provided by Signify Australia through domestic retailers.
