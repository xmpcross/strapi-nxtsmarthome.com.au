The Philips Hue Akari Downlight is a recessed smart LED luminaire designed for flush ceiling installation. According to Philips Hue, the fitting draws 8.5 watts and outputs up to 800 lumens of light in its White and Colour Ambiance configuration. It provides tunable white light spanning 2,000K to 6,500K alongside up to 16 million colours. Sized for standard 90mm cutouts, the Akari features an IP44 rating on the exposed face, making it suitable for moisture-prone indoor areas including bathrooms and laundries. Out of the box, the unit operates via Bluetooth through the Philips Hue mobile app for basic dimming and colour selection, or via Zigbee when paired with an optional Philips Hue Bridge to unlock system-wide routines and cross-platform integrations.

## Who it suits
The Akari suits Australian homeowners and renovators looking to install flush downlights that tie directly into an existing Philips Hue ecosystem. If you already rely on Hue switches, motion sensors, or portable lamps, using Hue-native downlights ensures consistent colour reproduction and unified control without needing third-party smart dimmer modules behind your wall plates. Its IP44 splash-proof rating also makes it practical for wet areas, allowing matching ambient lighting throughout living areas, kitchens, and ensuites.

Renters or those wanting a quick DIY retrofit without roof space access should look elsewhere, as replacing fixed downlights is less flexible than installing standard screw-in or bayonet smart globes. It is also less suitable for buyers who do not intend to purchase a Hue Bridge; relying purely on Bluetooth eliminates out-of-home control, Matter bridging, and Apple Home functionality.

## Australian notes
Philips Hue lists the Akari as IC-4 rated for Australian installations, meaning it is rated for safe contact with building insulation in standard ceiling cavities. The fitting comes pre-wired with an Australian standard 2-pin plug, allowing straightforward plug-in connection where ceiling socket outlets already exist. However, if your ceiling lacks existing plug bases, hardwiring or installing new power sockets is generally work for a licensed electrician; check your state's electrical safety regulator before proceeding. When paired with a Philips Hue Bridge connected to your local network, the Akari links with Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Apple Home, SmartThings, and Home Assistant, supporting Matter bridging provided by the Hue ecosystem.
