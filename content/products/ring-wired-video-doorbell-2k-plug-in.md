The Ring Wired Video Doorbell 2K Plug-In is a mains-powered video doorbell designed to eliminate the maintenance of rechargeable batteries. Ring Australia states that the unit delivers 2K video resolution with up to 6x digital zoom and a 140-degree horizontal by 140-degree vertical field of view, providing a 1:1 head-to-toe perspective of visitors and doorstep deliveries. Power is supplied via an included indoor adapter equipped with a six-metre cable, though Ring notes the doorbell can alternatively connect to an 8–24 VAC supply using a DIN rail transformer.

## Who it suits
According to retailer listings and technical specifications, this doorbell suits households already invested in the Amazon Alexa smart-home ecosystem. It links with Echo speakers to broadcast chime alerts and can stream live camera feeds directly to compatible Echo Show screens. It also suits properties where running a thin power cable through an exterior wall or doorframe into a nearby indoor electrical outlet is straightforward, removing the need to take the unit offline for charging.

However, households seeking local footage storage should look at alternatives. Ring Australia specifies that saving and reviewing video history requires an ongoing Ring Protect subscription, as there is no onboard microSD card slot or local hub support. It is also unsuitable for smart homes built around Apple Home, Google Home, Matter, or SmartThings, as Ring does not list compatibility for these platforms. Furthermore, the unit will not trigger traditional internal mechanical or electronic chimes.

## Australian notes
The doorbell connects to standard Australian indoor power outlets using the included plug-in power supply. If you opt for hardwiring into an 8–24 VAC DIN rail transformer instead, check your state or territory electrical safety regulator for licensed electrical work requirements. If you are renting, drilling mounting holes or running cables through exterior walls or window frames may require landlord permission under your state or territory residential tenancy laws.

Ring states the unit carries an IP55 weather-resistance rating and operates in temperatures ranging from -20°C to 50°C, accommodating typical Australian weather extremes. Wi-Fi connectivity is strictly single-band 2.4 GHz (802.11 b/g/n), meaning your home network must broadcast a dedicated 2.4 GHz signal to your front door. In Australia, the product includes a one-year limited manufacturer warranty, lifetime theft protection, and standard consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law.
