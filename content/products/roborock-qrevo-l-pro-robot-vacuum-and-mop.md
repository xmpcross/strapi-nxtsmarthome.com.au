The Roborock Qrevo L Pro is a robot vacuum and mop combo paired with an automated all-in-one docking station. Roborock rates the vacuum motor at 18,500 Pa suction and equips the chassis with an anti-tangle roller and side brush designed to minimise hair wrapping. For mopping, the machine uses dual spinning pads operating at up to 200 rpm. When it docks, the base station automatically empties dust into a 2.7-litre disposable bag, replenishes the internal water tank from a 4-litre reservoir, washes the mops with 75°C hot water, and dries them using 45°C warm air to inhibit bacterial growth and odours.

## Who it suits
This machine suits pet owners and busy households with floor plans composed largely of hard surfaces, such as floorboards, tiles, or hybrid planks, alongside low-pile rugs. The dual rotating pads apply continuous pressure to lift dried household spills, while the 10 mm automated mop lift prevents damp pads from dragging across thin rugs during mixed cleaning runs. Multi-storey homes can also make good use of the LiDAR navigation system, which stores up to four independent level layouts without requiring repeat mapping runs.

Households dominated by plush, thick carpeting should consider alternatives, as the 10 mm lift may not clear deeper fibres completely. Furthermore, because the mop pads are fixed under the robot chassis rather than mounted on an extending arm, the unit cannot wash right up against skirting boards and kitchen kickboards, leaving perimeter edges for occasional manual touch-ups.

## Australian notes
Official Australian stock includes an Australian three-pin plug for standard 230V mains outlets. When purchased from authorised local retailers such as Bunnings or Big W, the robot is backed by a two-year manufacturer warranty.

The dock measures 340 mm wide, 488 mm deep, and 518 mm high, and it requires vertical clearance above the unit so you can remove the clean and dirty water containers. Network connectivity is strictly 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, meaning users with combined mesh Wi-Fi setups may need to temporarily pause the 5 GHz band during initial device pairing. Smart home voice control integrates with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Apple Siri Shortcuts via the Roborock app.
