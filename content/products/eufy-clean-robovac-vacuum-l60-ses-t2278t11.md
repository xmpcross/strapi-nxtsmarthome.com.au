The eufy Clean Robovac L60 SES (model T2278T11) is a 2-in-1 hybrid robot vacuum cleaner paired with an auto-empty docking station. According to eufy AU, the unit delivers up to 5,000 Pa of suction, supported by BoostIQ technology to automatically increase power when transitioning onto carpeted surfaces. Navigation is managed via an iPath LiDAR system using AI.Map 2.0, which allows users to save multi-floor layouts and configure virtual boundaries. The self-empty dock evacuates the onboard 350 ml dustbin using 17,000 Pa of suction into a 2.5 L sealed dust bag, while an internal mechanical blade automatically trims wrapped hair off the roller brush.

## Who it suits

This system suits pet owners and households dealing with hair shedding who want hands-off dust disposal without regularly clearing the main roller brush by hand. With a documented 20 mm obstacle climbing threshold and an attachable passive mop featuring app-controlled water flow, it can handle routine maintenance across hard floors and standard floor transitions. Australian retailer listings cite a 2,600 mAh battery that provides up to 120 minutes of runtime per charge.

However, households requiring heavy-duty floor washing should look elsewhere. The mopping system uses a static drag cloth rather than spinning or vibrating pads, and the pad cannot lift when traversing carpet. Users must define no-mop boundaries in the app or manually detach the water tank assembly to prevent wet carpets. Additionally, obstacle navigation relies purely on LiDAR and front physical bumper sensors rather than optical recognition cameras, meaning small floor clutter like charging cords or stray socks can cause stalls. Prospective buyers should also note that manufacturer records list the L60 line as discontinued.

## Australian notes

Retail shipments from Australian stores include a standard Australian mains plug. Official technical manuals confirm the dock has a universal 100–240V rating (50/60 Hz), making it directly compatible with standard domestic 230–240V outlets.

The L60 supports both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi frequencies, easing setup on mixed modern home networks. For voice integration, it connects with Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa. Apple Home, Matter, and Samsung SmartThings are not supported, and Home Assistant relies entirely on unofficial community add-ons. Australian stockists provide a 12-month manufacturer warranty, and standard app scheduling functions do not require a paid subscription.
