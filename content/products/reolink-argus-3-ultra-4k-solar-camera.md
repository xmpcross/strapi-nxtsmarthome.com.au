The Reolink Argus 3 Ultra is a wire-free outdoor security camera that captures video at 4K (8-megapixel) resolution. According to Reolink, the camera is powered by an internal rechargeable battery paired with an external solar panel, allowing it to operate outdoors without fixed electrical wiring. It connects over dual-band 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi and features built-in spotlights that provide full-colour night footage alongside standard infrared night vision. Motion detection is handled locally via a passive infrared sensor and on-device processing, which Reolink states can distinguish people, vehicles, and pets from incidental background motion. Reolink also includes two-way audio, a built-in siren, and an IP65 weatherproof housing designed for exterior domestic use.

## Who it suits
This camera suits homeowners and renters seeking high-resolution perimeter surveillance without the complexity of hiring an electrician or running network cables. Reolink specifies that motion clips can be saved locally to a microSD card without an ongoing subscription, making it suitable for buyers who prefer local data retention over mandatory cloud fees. It also suits properties with reliable Wi-Fi reach in outdoor areas, where users want flexibility to mount cameras on fences, sheds, or exterior walls.

Conversely, buyers looking for 24/7 continuous recording should consider wired alternatives. Because it is battery-operated, Reolink designs the standalone camera primarily for event-based capture to preserve battery reserves. Those committed to the Apple HomeKit ecosystem should also note that Reolink does not provide native Apple Home or Matter support for this model, though it does integrate with Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa.

## Australian notes
Because the Argus 3 Ultra relies entirely on an internal battery and a low-voltage USB-C solar panel, it avoids mains voltage installation requirements. However, renters should check their residential tenancy agreement before drilling mounting brackets into exterior surfaces.

When positioning the camera, ensure the solar panel faces north or receives unshaded sunlight daily, as winter shading will reduce recharging performance. Homeowners should also verify local council regulations and state surveillance devices legislation to ensure camera angles respect neighbouring boundaries and privacy expectations. Network setup requires an active 2.4GHz or 5GHz Wi-Fi connection at the mounting position. Local purchases through authorised Australian retailers are backed by statutory consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law.
