The WiZ Colour Smart Downlight is a 90mm recessed LED ceiling fixture that provides up to 800 lumens of brightness across 16 million colours and tunable white temperatures from 2200K to 6500K. According to manufacturer specifications from WiZ Connected, the fitting consumes 8W of power, provides a 100-degree beam angle, and communicates directly over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth without requiring a dedicated hardware hub or bridge. It is also Matter certified, allowing cross-platform local and cloud integration.

## Who it suits
This downlight suits homeowners and renters with existing 90mm to 100mm ceiling cut-outs who want flexible colour and white lighting without being locked into a single smart-home ecosystem. Because WiZ Connected includes Matter certification alongside native cloud integrations, it fits households running Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings, or local Home Assistant platforms.

It also suits those looking for automated presence sensing without adding external physical sensors, provided they install multiple lights. WiZ Connected notes that its SpaceSense feature uses Wi-Fi signal disturbances to detect movement, though this requires at least two compatible WiZ fixtures in the same room. Conversely, anyone planning to retain existing trailing-edge or leading-edge hardware wall dimmer switches should skip this fitting, as WiZ explicitly states it is incompatible with in-wall dimmers.

## Australian notes
According to retailer listings from JB Hi-Fi and Green Earth Lighting, the Australian model is supplied with a pre-wired 0.8-metre flex and a standard three-pin plug. If you already have 240V surface sockets (plug bases) installed inside your ceiling cavity, you can plug this fitting directly into the existing socket without requiring wiring work. However, if your ceiling has hardwired lighting or no accessible surface sockets, fixed wiring work is generally work for a licensed electrician; check your state's electrical safety regulator for specific requirements.

WiZ Connected specifies that the unit operates on Australian 220–240V AC mains at 50/60 Hz and holds a Class II double-insulated rating. The fitting requires a dedicated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi connection (802.11 b/g/n) during operation, as 5 GHz bands are unsupported. For bathroom or outdoor installations, WiZ Connected notes that while the front face has an IP54 water-resistance rating, the recessed body is IP40 and must only sit in dry ceiling cavities. Australian retailers provide a two-year manufacturer warranty.
