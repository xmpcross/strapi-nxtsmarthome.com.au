The Philips Hue Smart Plug (model 929003051001) is an Australian three-pin adapter designed to add switching capabilities to non-smart lighting and appliances. According to Signify, the plug operates as an on/off switch within the Hue system, letting you control connected fixtures via the Philips Hue smartphone app or compatible voice platforms. It incorporates dual wireless protocols: Bluetooth for direct, in-room smartphone pairing, and Zigbee for integration with a Hue Bridge or third-party Zigbee coordinators.

## Who it suits
Philips positions this smart plug primarily as a practical alternative for table lamps, floor lamps, or decorative fixtures where fitting a Hue smart bulb is impractical or prevented by unusual bulb fittings. It suits households that already use the Philips Hue platform and want standard plug-in lights to turn on and off in tandem with their existing Hue scenes and accessory switches. Because it can connect directly through Bluetooth without any central equipment, it also suits renters and beginners looking for basic in-room app switching.

However, households wanting dimming capabilities should look elsewhere, as the unit is strictly a mechanical on/off relay and cannot dim lights. It also lacks energy-metering hardware, meaning you cannot monitor electricity draw or calculate appliance running costs. Furthermore, those wanting remote out-of-home control, Apple Home integration, or advanced automation routines must pair the plug to an optional Philips Hue Bridge rather than relying solely on direct Bluetooth connectivity.

## Australian notes
The smart plug is configured for Australian and New Zealand wall outlets with a Type I three-pin plug. According to the manufacturer's Australian technical documentation, it is rated for 220–240V AC at up to 2,300W (10A), allowing it to switch domestic lamps and standard small household appliances safely. Because it is an external plug-in adapter, it requires no electrical wiring or licensed installation work.

At 60 mm wide and 64 mm high, the casing is relatively compact, though on some closely spaced double power points the square profile can partially obscure the switch or adjacent socket. The device does not connect directly to 2.4GHz Wi-Fi; instead, it relies on Bluetooth or Zigbee. When paired into a Zigbee network via a Hue Bridge or platforms like Home Assistant, it functions as a mains-powered Zigbee repeater to strengthen signal mesh throughout the home. Philips Hue covers the plug with a two-year warranty in Australia.
