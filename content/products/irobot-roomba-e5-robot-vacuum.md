The iRobot Roomba E5 (model e515000) is a dedicated robotic vacuum cleaner designed for automated dry floor maintenance. According to iRobot Australia, the unit features a three-stage cleaning system equipped with dual multi-surface rubber brushes, an edge-sweeping brush angled at 27 degrees, and suction rated at five times the power of the Roomba 600 series. Measuring 37.8 centimetres in diameter and 9.14 centimetres in height, it navigates under and around household furniture using an iAdapt sensor suite paired with Dirt Detect sensors that prompt repeat passes over concentrated debris. A washable dust container collects dirt, and an 1800 mAh lithium-ion battery provides up to 90 minutes of cleaning runtime before the robot returns autonomously to its included Home Base.

## Who it suits
According to its technical specifications, the Roomba E5 suits households seeking basic automated floor cleaning across carpets and hard surfaces without complex digital setups. The dual rubber brushes are designed to resist hair tangles better than traditional bristle rolls, making it practical for pet owners. Control is handled via the free iRobot HOME app or voice commands through Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, requiring no ongoing subscription fees.

However, buyers with larger floor plans or multi-room layouts may find its navigation limiting. The E5 relies on reactive bounce navigation rather than camera-based vSLAM or LiDAR mapping. Consequently, it cannot generate persistent digital maps, establish virtual keep-out zones, or target specific individual rooms. It does not support mopping, nor does it work with an automatic self-emptying base station, requiring users to manually empty the internal bin. Furthermore, official Australian channels indicate the model is discontinued from direct manufacturer sale, meaning remaining availability is primarily through third-party marketplace sellers.

## Australian notes
According to the Australian product listing, the Roomba E5 comes supplied with a standard Australian AC line cord for the Home Base charging dock. Exact operating voltage ranges and standby power draw are not specified on the local listing. Network connectivity is restricted strictly to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi frequencies; dual-band home routers will need to broadcast a compatible 2.4 GHz band during setup, as 5 GHz networks are not supported.

Smart home integration is limited natively to Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Official support is not provided for Apple Home, Matter, or Samsung SmartThings, though unofficial integrations exist within Home Assistant. For local consumer support, iRobot Australia provides a one-year manufacturer limited warranty that covers both the robot and the battery.
