The Amazon Echo (4th Gen) is a spherical mains-powered smart speaker measuring 144 mm by 144 mm by 133 mm and weighing 970 grams. According to JB Hi-Fi and manufacturer specifications, the internal acoustic setup consists of a 3.0-inch (76.2 mm) neodymium woofer and dual 0.8-inch (20 mm) front-firing tweeters backed by Dolby audio processing. Alongside standard voice-assistant capabilities, the device features an integrated Zigbee hub, Bluetooth Low Energy Mesh, a Thread border router, and an onboard temperature sensor. A 3.5 mm auxiliary socket on the rear can be configured for either audio input or output, according to GadgetGuy.

## Who it suits
This unit suits households built around the Amazon Alexa platform that need room audio combined with a centralised smart home controller. Because it contains built-in Zigbee and Matter controller capabilities, users can directly pair and manage compatible sensors, light bulbs, and smart plugs without purchasing standalone brand bridges. Retailer and community documentation also notes that it can link into Samsung SmartThings and Home Assistant setups through Matter bridging or Alexa cloud integrations.

Conversely, users relying on Apple Home or Google Home should skip this device, as neither platform is natively supported. While the speaker features a rounded enclosure, testing notes from SoundGuys and Pickr observe that sound output is directional—firing forward and upward—rather than true 360-degree audio. Hardware controls on top are restricted to volume adjustments, microphone mute, and an action button, meaning media playback cannot be paused from the physical chassis. Those needing a portable speaker for verandahs or outdoor areas will also need to consider other models, as it does not include an internal battery.

## Australian notes
Units supplied for the Australian market come with an external 30 W power adapter (18 V / 1.67 A) featuring a proprietary DC barrel plug and a standard Australian two-pin mains connector. Network connectivity relies on dual-band 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi (802.11a/b/g/n/ac), though Amazon device specifications note that ad-hoc peer-to-peer Wi-Fi networks are unsupported.

Amazon provides a standard one-year limited manufacturer warranty through local retailers like JB Hi-Fi and Mobileciti; for questions regarding statutory consumer guarantees, check with the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission. Core functionality—including voice queries, alarms, timers, and basic radio—operates without recurring fees, though on-demand music streaming through services such as Spotify or Amazon Music requires separate paid subscriptions.
