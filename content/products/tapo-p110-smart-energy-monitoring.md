The TP-Link Tapo P110 is an indoor smart plug that sits between a standard Australian wall socket and an appliance. According to TP-Link Australia, it provides real-time power tracking, historical usage graphs across daily, monthly, and yearly intervals, and an integrated electricity bill calculator within the free Tapo application. It also incorporates scheduling presets, an away mode, a countdown timer, and an automatic overload shut-off feature housed in UL94-V0 flame-retardant polycarbonate casing.

## Who it suits

This unit suits homeowners and renters seeking to monitor energy usage on standard household items such as entertainment units, portable heaters, or office equipment. Because it requires no electrical installation, renters can deploy it without modifying fixtures. It integrates directly into setups using Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, or Samsung SmartThings over a local Wi-Fi connection without a paid subscription.

However, households invested in Apple Home or those requiring the Matter smart home standard should skip this model, as TP-Link reserves Matter support for the distinct P110M variant. It is also unsuitable for high-draw machinery or appliances requiring more than 10 A, and it should not be deployed outdoors or in damp areas due to a lack of weatherproofing.

## Australian notes

According to TP-Link Australia, the Tapo P110 features an Australian 3-pin plug rated for 220–240 V at 50/60 Hz, supported by local SAA and Regulatory Compliance Mark (RCM) certifications. Its maximum operating capacity is rated at 10 A, 2300 W, or a 1/10 HP motor. Users connecting heating elements or workshop tools must ensure their device does not exceed this 10 A rating; always consult manufacturer specifications or check your state's electrical safety regulator regarding appliance load limits.

For connectivity, the socket functions strictly on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks (IEEE 802.11b/g/n) and does not support 5 GHz bands or captive portals. Official local retail packages sold through Australian retailers such as JB Hi-Fi, Bunnings Warehouse, and Officeworks are backed by TP-Link Australia's two-year limited warranty for smart power devices.
