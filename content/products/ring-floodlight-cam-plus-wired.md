The Ring Floodlight Cam Wired Plus is an outdoor security camera paired with two integrated LED floodlights. Designed for perimeter surveillance across areas such as driveways, garages, or backyards, the unit combines 1080p HD video recording with 2,000 lumens of light output at a 3000K colour temperature. Ring Australia specifies that the camera provides a 140-degree horizontal field of view, colour night vision, and 270-degree motion detection with customisable motion zones. It also incorporates two-way audio with noise cancellation and a 105 dB siren that can be triggered remotely through the mobile application.

## Who it suits

This device suits homeowners seeking an all-in-one floodlight and camera fixture who already operate within the Amazon Alexa ecosystem. Because it supports Alexa voice commands and compatible smart displays, it fits properties set up around Amazon's smart home platform. It also suits buyers who prefer constant mains power over taking down cameras to recharge batteries every few months.

Conversely, renters should consider skipping this model because it cannot be plugged into a standard outdoor power point and requires permanent physical wiring. Buyers seeking local recording without ongoing fees will also want to look elsewhere, as the unit lacks microSD or network storage options; saving, downloading, or reviewing video requires an ongoing paid Ring subscription. Homes relying primarily on Apple Home or Google Home will find no native integration.

## Australian notes

The Floodlight Cam Wired Plus is rated for 100 to 240 VAC mains power and comes without a standard plug. Fixed installations connected directly to mains wiring are generally work for a licensed electrician; check your state or territory electrical safety regulator for specific rules. Furthermore, if your camera's field of view captures areas outside your property boundary, check your local council and state surveillance privacy regulations regarding outdoor cameras.

For connectivity, the unit operates strictly on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks (802.11 b/g/n) and does not support 5 GHz bands, meaning you must ensure adequate 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi signal reaches the mounting position outside your home. In terms of local consumer protection, Ring Australia offers a one-year limited manufacturer warranty on parts alongside a lifetime theft replacement policy, sitting alongside statutory consumer guarantees provided under the Australian Consumer Law.
