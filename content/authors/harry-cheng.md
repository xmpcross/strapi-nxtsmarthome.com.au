Harry covers the practical end: getting hardware working, keeping it working,
and the energy questions that decide whether a device pays for itself. His
guides follow the standard in [How we test](/how-we-test/) — running costs are
worked from real tariffs rather than manufacturer figures, and anything touching
fixed wiring says plainly when a licensed electrician is required.
