Adrian covers buying guides and the hub and platform comparisons — the questions
that come before a purchase, where getting it wrong is expensive and hard to
undo. His guides work to the standard set out in [How we test](/how-we-test/):
no star rating on a device that has not been used, and no claim about wiring,
tenancy or privacy law without a source behind it.
